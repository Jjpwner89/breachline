The creator, 2026-11-23, on parking the houses' campaigning season: "keep possible for later. let's put those in a parked feature log for me to consider after my playtest".

The log is `PARKED_FEATURES.md` at the root of the RTS repo. It started with: the campaigning season, a fallow winter as the default (playable now via `?winter=hard`), chained hauls, touch controls and a Claude director.

**Why:** a parked idea isn't a cut idea. The creator wants to look back over them after playing, so they mustn't get lost in chat or the design notes.

**How to apply:**
- When the creator parks something, add it to the log: what it is, why it was parked (their words), and what it would need.
- After each playtest, offer to go through the log with them.
- Move an item to ROADMAP.md or DESIGN_NOTES.md when it's taken up. Delete it when it's dropped for good.
- Never build from the log without the creator's go.
