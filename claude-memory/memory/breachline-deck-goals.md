Every deck should stick to its own goals, and need not be the most competitive build. Every deck keeps some simple, modest cards, so commons are always present.

Starter decks should:
- be good enough to clear their campaign;
- introduce as many of the colour's features as possible while staying on theme;
- value consistent playability over variety, though variety is still good when all else is equal.

**Ownership (2026-11-11):** Claude is responsible for making sure the final balance decisions and the new 40-card decks reach the entire game. That covers the engine and card data, card texts, the starter, theme and campaign enemy decks, faction pools, the deck builder rules, tests, guides and the tutorial.

**Why:** the creator said so directly after the round 8 balance pass and the 40-card deck work.

**How to apply:** when locking the sim patches in, sweep every file that names a card, deck size or copy limit. Check starters against their campaign clear rate as well as the round robin. See [[breachline-project]] and [[quick-balance-check]].
