For the RTS's 3D art (2026-11-18), the user preferred Quaternius "Ultimate Fantasy RTS" buildings over modular kits (Quaternius Medieval Village MegaKit, Kenney), because they "feel like more complete assets". They like Quaternius for props and the Quaternius 2018 knight, want gear swappable per unit, and asked for helmets and gear to be refitted to whichever body is chosen. No final pick yet.

**Why:** they want the game to look finished, and they found first-pass asset searches poor (same with an earlier soundtrack hunt).

**How to apply:** when sourcing models, scan broadly first (all styles, free and paid, past page one), then narrow. Favour complete, finished models (ideally with upgrade stages) over kits that have to be assembled. The building list can bend to fit good assets: the game must keep gathering buildings and a layered resource system (raw → refined → finished goods), but the exact workshops (ropemaker, bowyer and so on) are not fixed. The comparison page is the "RTS Model Lineup" artifact. See [[rts-project]].

Checked 2026-11-19: the free (Standard) Quaternius "Modular Character Outfits – Fantasy" has only the peasant and ranger outfits. The knight, noble, king and queen in its preview are in the paid Source version. Its peasant clothes fit a "Regular" body that the free "Universal Base Characters" lacks (that pack has only the muscular "Superhero" bodies, which poke through). Both packs are slimmer and textured, unlike the chunky flat-coloured game people. Don't re-download them hoping for armour.

2026-11-19: soldier limb animation is **on hold**. The creator wants to see what people models Codex makes (BRIEFS.md section 7) before deciding whether to keep the Quaternius modular skeleton. Don't bake walk cycles or rig work onto the current soldiers until they decide.
