# Sound credits

Most sounds here are CC0 (public domain): credit isn't required and is given as a courtesy. Some of the spell-stage, impact and combat sounds further down are CC-BY, which costs nothing but asks for the creator to be named, and they are credited there. The first set came from OpenGameArt.org, downloaded on 2026-11-10 and picked on the polish page. The spell, heal, ward and curse sounds were downloaded and picked on 2026-11-11 from the sound board: they come from OpenGameArt.org, lentikula's packs on itch.io and the Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL, github.com/sgossner/VCSL). Those were trimmed, faded and levelled. Sources in .wav, .flac or .mp3 were converted to AAC (.mp4).

## Spell stages

A spell thrown across the table charges, launches and flies, and each stage layers a shared air sound under the element's own. These were picked on the spell sound board on 2026-11-11, from packs already downloaded for the sound board, and trimmed, faded, levelled and converted like the rest. No charge fitted Vigil or Gnosis, and no launch Gnosis, so those stages play the air alone.
