# Card audit: small numbers

Done on 2026-11-05, when the trailing zero came off every number in the game. Each value was set by the card's character and its alignment's tendencies, not by a rounding rule. Equal attack and health is fine, because the creator found in play that bigger attackers one-shot most units anyway. Balance was allowed to break, and everything that broke is catalogued below for the card build-out.

Summoner life went from 150 to 15. Costs and land wants didn't change.

## Gap catalogue

### Where balance moved

Scripted bot on both sides, 20,000 games per column. The bot doesn't position units to help each other and rarely sacrifices well, so white and black may be better in human hands.

| Deck | Old numbers, 11 lands | Small numbers, 11 lands | Small numbers, 8 lands (current) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Vigil | 38% | 46% | 40% |
| Fervor | 52% | 62% | 57% |
| Gnosis | 49% | 36% | 48% |
| Dominion | 50% | 40% | 39% |
| Genesis | 62% | 65% | 66% |

Games last about 22 turns at 8 lands and 23 at 11. One-hit kills between similarly priced units went from 27% to 40%, and average hits to kill from 2.0 to 1.8. The first player wins about 47%.

### Holes by alignment

Counted on the 56 cards in `main`, including the ten below. `CARD_PROPOSALS.md` has cards for each of these.

- **Gnosis (9 cards):** averages 1.8 attack per unit. Nothing costs more than 4, so it has no finisher. Its deck is cheap, so it recovered once decks dropped to 8 lands.
- **Dominion (7 cards):** only 5 units, none at 4 or 5 mana, and no instants.
- **Fervor (13 cards):** the strongest attack in the game. Direct damage is a listed strength, and Firebolt is the only such card. Nothing at 6 mana.
- **Genesis (10 cards):** "very large units" is a listed strength, but nothing costs more than 5. No 1-cost or 4-cost unit.
- **Vigil (14 cards):** no 5-cost card. Its only 2-cost unit has 1 attack. Stand together and Guard each appear on one card, so neither can carry a deck.
- **Neutral (3 cards):** only 1 unit.

### Thin keywords

Eight of the 12 keywords appear on only one card: Stand together, Guard, Frenzy, Decoy, Grows, Regrow, Overrun and Hunt.

Of the 35 units, 15 have only passive keywords, 10 have active abilities, 1 has a summon effect and 9 have nothing.

### Cards with nothing

Cards costing 1 or 2 can have no ability and be fine (the creator's guideline, not a hard rule). So Militia spearman, Goblin cutter, Gravebound thrall, Orc raider and Young summoner can stay plain.

Three plain units costing 3 or more are where abilities can add the most. Sellsword also costs 3, but stays plain and moves to neutral, at the creator's request. The ideas are proposals, to take or leave.

| Card | Cost | Stats | Passive idea |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oath knight | 3 | 3 / 5 | Steadfast: +1 attack if she hasn't moved this turn. Fits "No one ordered her to stay" |
| Orc warchief | 4 | 5 / 5 | Warband: adjacent allies get +1 attack |
| Hill giant | 5 | 5 / 8 | Stomp: its attacks also deal 1 to enemies next to the target |

### Outclassed cards

Same or lower cost, at least the same stats, and more going for it:

- **Goblin cutter (2 / 1)** by Militia spearman and Gravebound thrall (both 2 / 2).
- **Orc warchief (5 / 5)** by Sunmane lancer (5 / 6, with Swift and an ability).
- **Hill giant (5 / 8)** by Elder Herd bull (6 / 9, with Overrun).

## Land share

Decided on 2026-11-06: 35%, which is 14 lands in 40 cards. The 24-card starter decks round to 8 lands (33%), and the deck builder's advice now uses 35%.

To make room, each starter deck gained 3 cards, all copies of cards it already ran:

| Deck | Lands | Added |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Oath of the Sunmane | 8 Vigil | Water carrier, Temple cleric, Oath sentinel |
| The Warhorde | 7 Fervor, 1 Gnosis | Cinder Clan raiders, Orc raider, Cinder berserker |
| The Lantern Conclave | 7 Gnosis, 1 Vigil | Street conjurer, Lantern arcanist, Ambrel lampwright |
| The Ashen Court | 6 Dominion, 2 Fervor | Gravebound thrall, Bone harvester, Raise the dead |
| The Elder Herd | 6 Genesis, 2 Fervor | Walking sapling, Thornwarden saboteur, Great owl |

Land drops in bot games, 20,000 games each:

| Lands | Share | 2 or fewer lands in play as their sixth turn begins | Average lands in play as their tenth turn begins |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 11 of 24 | 46% | 6% | 6.4 |
| 8 of 24 | 33% | 25% | 4.8 |

At 4.8 lands on turn 10, the 6- and 7-mana cards arrive late, and wanting 3 lands of a colour is harder to meet. The 40-card decks will need 9 or more different non-land cards at 3 copies each. Black and green have 8 each with neutrals, so a one-colour 40-card deck in either can't be filled until the build-out adds cards.

These are estimates for 40-card decks at other land counts, for the first player, counting the card drawn that turn:

| Lands | Share | Stuck on 2 or fewer at their sixth turn |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 16 of 40 | 40% | 10% |
| 15 of 40 | 38% | 14% |
| 14 of 40 | 35% | 18% |

## Build focus coverage

Counted on 2026-11-09 against the build focuses in `DESIGN_NOTES.md`: the 82 built cards, plus proposals 31 to 42 from `CARD_PROPOSALS.md`. A card counts toward a focus only when it actively does that thing or rewards it, and many count toward two. Ten plain cards count toward none, which is fine for cheap units. In the table, "2 +1" means 2 built cards and 1 proposed.

A 40-card deck has 26 non-land cards, about 9 different cards at 3 copies each. So a focus needs about 8 supporting cards within one or two colours before a deck can be built around it.

Ranged attackers wasn't on the draft focus list. It's added here because it's a listed Gnosis strength.

What the counts show:

- **Ready to build a deck around:** big creatures, mostly in green and red. Holding ground in white. Direct damage and racing in red.
- **Close:**
  - Control, healing, ranged attackers, standing together, and ambush and movement each have 5 to 10 cards.
  - Most of them sit in a single colour, so they don't yet stretch across colours the way the focuses should.
- **Thin:**
  - Sacrifice and death triggers, and raising from the discard pile: 2 to 3 black cards each. The only death trigger is the proposed Porcelain-visored retainer.
  - Card flow: no card yet adds cards to your hand.
- **Empty or nearly empty:**
  - Tokens: one payoff, Clearing the channel, and no card that summons units.
  - Tile effects: Cinderfall's splash only.
  - Faction loyalty: no card rewards staying within a faction.
  - Tokens and tile effects are new mechanics and need their first cards. Faction loyalty needs a rule decided before any cards can reward it.
- **Focuses stuck in one colour:**
  - Ranged is all blue.
  - Raising from the discard pile and sacrifice are all black.
  - Racing is nearly all red.
  - Standing together has no green herd cards.
  - Equipment has no built red card.

Suggested priorities for the next card batch:

1. **Tokens:** summoners in white, red, green and black, plus one or two payoffs.
2. **Sacrifice and death triggers:** in black, red and white.
3. **Tile effects and spells aimed at a tile:** in blue, red, green and black.
4. **New keywords seeded into focuses that are close to ready:** Flanking, Holding ground and Ambush.

Vigil and Fervor got the fewest cards in the last batch, and tokens would give both of them cards.
