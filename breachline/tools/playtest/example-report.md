# Red (ptRush), Sonnet 5.5 high — lost T31 (stuck on 5 lands T8–T28; the bot was a ranged Gnosis deck)
- Hired: "most interesting, mostly a trap". The 6/6 for 3 with 2 upkeep ate half the mana and blocked the 4-drop; ranged units chip it with no strike-back. The 4/4 for 2 with 1 upkeep was better. No decision after summoning. Suggest upkeep 1 or cost 4–5, and a way to release.
- Eager (move 1 on arrival, no attack): flat. Suggest attack-not-move, or +1 attack on arrival.
- Surge: a real X decision, good late. The X=0..4 lines for every target clutter the move list.
- Overreach: a fine discount; the stray never happened (few neighbours), so the drawback was invisible.
- Momentum: drawn too late.
- Overall not fun: melee rush out-traded by ranged control with few cheap answers.
- UI: upkeep payment isn't logged; Swift isn't explained; adjacency (orthogonal only) isn't stated.
