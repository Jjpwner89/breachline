# New themed decks, 2026-11-11 to 2026-11-12

Six decks built from the current cards, plus two new keywords (Venom and Overwatch) and pushes that set off tiles. Each deck started as close to its theme as it could get, then moved toward balance a few cards at a time. All six went into the game as theme decks on 2026-11-12, with the creator's card picks.

Every number is the deck's win rate against the 15 existing starter and theme decks, from 600 bot games on shared seeds (about ±2 points). The target is 45% to 55%.

## The new mechanics

- **Venom:** any damage it deals to a unit destroys it, including when it strikes back.
- **Overwatch:** if it didn't move or attack on its own turn, it strikes the first enemy to end a move within its reach on the enemy's turn. Once per turn.
- **Pushes set off tiles:** a unit pushed onto a tile is affected as if it had walked there, so pushing into fire or thorns now hurts.

For testing, the keywords were first lent to seven existing cards. After the card review, the creator kept these:

- **Court assassin:** 2 / 2 with Ambush and Venom, losing Hunt. Lift −1.6, a little under the uncommon band.
- **Focus-rifle marksman:** gains Overwatch. Lift +1.2.
- **Thornwarden goblin scout:** gains Overwatch. Lift +0.5.
- **Shutter-window assassin:** Hunt and Overwatch, losing Longshot. That tested −3.4, well under the rare band, so the creator also made it 3 mana and 3 / 3 (−0.9).

Barrow rats, the Militia spearman and the Thornwarden trapper stayed as they were. Venom on a 1-mana common tested +4.9, and still +1.1 at 2 mana and 1 / 1.

## How the fully themed first versions did

| Deck | Colours | Idea | First version |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Giants' road | Green and red | Big bodies on a ramp | 54% |
| Levy and warband | White and red | Wide cheap army, Overwatch spearmen | 48% |
| First blood | Red and black | Cheap Venom attackers | 38% |
| Into the fire | Red and blue | Push enemies into fire | 26% |
| Funeral procession | Black and white | Units that come back or pay off from dying | 26% |
| Over the wall | Red and blue | Fliers and jumpers past the front line | 22% |

**What it tells us:**
- A fully themed deck usually has only about half a deck's worth of good cards. The rest is filler that fits the idea but loses.
- The biggest single cause was mana, not card choice. Every two-colour deck lost to the extra cost of off-colour cards, with land splits that didn't match the cards. Fixing the lands alone was worth 3 to 16 points.
- Mechanics that need a second step, like pushing into a tile you made first, play badly for the bot. The deck needs plain strong cards around the combo.
- For the deck builder: count how many good on-theme cards a player owns, fill the rest from the colour's best cards, and set the land split from the card colours.

## Where they stood before the picks

All six are now between 48% and 54%. Over the wall ended up mono red, and Funeral procession mono black. Both lost their second colour to the mana cost, and their themes come from the red fliers and the black dying-pays-off units.

That was with all seven lent keywords; the creator's picks came next.

## Sun and cinder, for the creator's brother (2026-11-12)

The creator asked for a deck their brother can play against them online and have a shot: pretty strong and fairly easy to use first, then fun, especially aimed spells with effects. It's a theme deck so anyone can pick it without owning the cards.

- **Plan:** white and red, 7 lands of each. Tough white units with simple rules hold the middle (Oath sentinel, Pride huntress, Oath knight, Sunmane lancer), red hitters push forward (Cinder berserker, Goblin firepot, one Hill giant), and 10 aimed spells clear the way: Scorch, Stormstrike, Cinderfall and Line of dawn.
- **Kept simple on purpose:** no Lit, pushes, equipment, sacrifice or Devotion. Most cards cost 2 to 4, and every spell is "aim it at enemies".
- **Strength:** 62% ± 2.6 over 1,400 games against the other 21 decks, the strongest deck in the game, above the usual 45% to 55% target on purpose, since it's meant to give a new player a chance against an experienced one. Best against The Lantern Conclave (83%), worst against Giants' road (38%) and Hearth and vow (42%).
