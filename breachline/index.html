Chronicle

Working rules

  • Each turn: draw, one land, play cards, then command units.
  • Each land short of a card's colour adds 1 to its cost.
  • Before turn 1 you can redraw your opening hand. Each redraw leaves you one card fewer.
  • New units start in your back row and wait a turn.
  • Units move, then attack or use an ability.
  • Damage stays. A target that survives strikes back if it can reach.
  • Only a unit on the enemy back row can hit the summoner.
  • Mana you leave unspent pays for instants on the enemy turn.
  • Reduce the enemy summoner from 15 to 0.

The table

  • The wax seal and its nails are a summoner's life.
  • Each gem is a land. Bright gems are mana left this turn.
  • A turn has a play step, then a command step.
  • A dimmed unit is waiting to act or has acted.
  • Small shields on a unit are its keywords and conditions.
  • The left plaque is attack, the right is health.
  • The bottom row is your back row, and the top row is the enemy's.
  • The discard pile is shuffled back in when your deck runs out.
  • Turn on Help to point at anything for a short explanation.
