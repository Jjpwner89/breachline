Working rules
  • Each turn: draw, one land, play cards, then command units.
  • Each land short of a card's colour adds 1 to its cost.
  • Once per turn you can discard a card and draw a new one.
  • New units start in your back row and wait a turn.
  • Units move, then attack or use an ability.
  • Damage stays. A target that survives strikes back if it can reach.
  • Only a unit on the enemy back row can hit the summoner.
  • Mana you leave unspent pays for instants on the enemy turn.
  • Reduce the enemy summoner from 150 to 0.
  • Towns and fortifications open in a later version.
