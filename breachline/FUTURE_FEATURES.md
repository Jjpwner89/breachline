# Future features

Card features, keywords and gameplay ideas that have been suggested but not built. Check this list when designing new cards or decks, and suggest an idea from it when it fits. Never add one to the game without the creator's approval.

Modes, screens and tools don't go here. Ideas the creator turned down are in DESIGN_NOTES.md under "Tried and set aside".

When an idea is built, delete it from this list.

## Keywords and card abilities

- **Kicker.** An optional extra cost for a bigger effect, such as a 2-mana spell that does more for 5. It gives ramp decks and late draws something to do. The creator finds it interesting but hard to balance.
- **Flash.** Summon a unit on the enemy's turn as a response, the way instants already answer attacks.
- **Cycling.** Pay 1 and discard the card to draw a new one. It smooths out 40-card decks and is cheap to build.
- **Face-down units, like Morph.** Summon a unit as a hidden 2 / 2 and flip it later for its cost. Bluffing suits play against a person; the bot would need teaching.
- **Venom on more cards.** Venom is in the game on the Court assassin. On a cheap common it tested far too strong (+4.9 on a 1-mana Barrow rats), so further Venom cards should be uncommon or above, or have low health.
- **Overwatch on more cards.** It's in the game on the Focus-rifle marksman, Thornwarden goblin scout and Shutter-window assassin. The creator ruled it out for the Militia spearman.

## Board and gameplay

- **Shrines on the map.** Middle tiles that give a card or mana each turn you hold them, so both sides have a reason to push forward. Campaign chapters could each have their own.
- **Hidden traps.** A face-down trap on a tile that the enemy can't see.

## Where these came from

Suggested on 2026-11-11 alongside Venom, Overwatch and pushes that set off tiles, which were built. The creator chose to build those three first and keep the rest for later.
