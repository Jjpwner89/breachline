# Future features

Card features, keywords and gameplay ideas that have been suggested but not built. Check this list when designing new cards or decks, and suggest an idea from it when it fits. Never add one to the game without the creator's approval.

Modes, screens and tools don't go here. Ideas the creator turned down are in DESIGN_NOTES.md under "Tried and set aside".

When an idea is built, delete it from this list.

## Keywords and card abilities

- **Flash.** Summon a unit on the enemy's turn as a response, the way instants already answer attacks.
- **Cycling.** Pay 1 and discard the card to draw a new one. It smooths out 40-card decks and is cheap to build.
- **Face-down units, like Morph.** Summon a unit as a hidden 2 / 2 and flip it later for its cost. Bluffing suits play against a person; the bot would need teaching.
- **Venom on more cards.** Venom is in the game on the Court assassin. On a cheap common it tested far too strong (+4.9 on a 1-mana Barrow rats), so further Venom cards should be uncommon or above, or have low health.
- **Overwatch on more cards.** It's in the game on the Focus-rifle marksman, Thornwarden goblin scout and Shutter-window assassin. The creator ruled it out for the Militia spearman. The Undercity edge-warden (#193) is proposed as its fourth card.
- **Evolving cards with evolving art (creator, 2026-11-15).** A unit meets a condition, such as paying an amount, healing a unit or attacking once, and levels up: a burst of light, and the card's art changes to its grown form, as a squire might become a knight. The creator likes it as a fun effect but worries it could be distracting, and doesn't want it built yet. Deep roots, Seasoned and Empower already grow units, so they would be natural first candidates. Each card would need two paintings.
- **Ledgered on more Dominion cards.** When the unit dies, you draw a card. It's proposed on the Doomed conscript (#199), a lesser Ashborn. The creator wants it kept in mind for later Dominion cards, but the current set doesn't need more.

- **A choice on extra-land cards (creator, 2026-11-15).** Cards that let you play an extra land, such as the Reedbank forager and the Wayfinder druid, have never done anything in the creator's games, and playtesters found them dead late. Give them a choice: play an extra land, or draw a card instead.
- **More card draw.** Land screw and flood are part of how the game plays, so there's no trading a card for a land. More cards that draw could make drawing a valued thing to do (creator, 2026-11-15).

## Board and gameplay
- **A Veiled marker that follows the unit (creator, 2026-11-15).** Playtesters couldn't tell when a Veiled unit can be targeted. Show Veiled on the board like a tile effect that moves with the unit.

- **Shrines on the map.** Middle tiles that give a card or mana each turn you hold them, so both sides have a reason to push forward. Campaign chapters could each have their own.
- **Hidden traps.** A face-down trap on a tile that the enemy can't see.
- **Stacking prestige tiers.** Prestige II, III and on, each a little harder than the last, unlocked by clearing the one before. Prestige has one tier for now (2026-11-13).

## Where these came from

Suggested on 2026-11-11 alongside Venom, Overwatch and pushes that set off tiles, which were built. The creator chose to build those three first and keep the rest for later.
