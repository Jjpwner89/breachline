# Breachline card art briefs: set 2

This file holds art briefs for 29 new images: 27 cards and 2 lands. Card names may still change.

## Instructions for the image generator

1. Read `.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md` first, and follow it if anything here seems unclear.
2. Generate one image per card, using the style block below plus that card's prompt.
3. Make each image a landscape PNG at about 3:2, for example 1536 x 1024.
4. Save each image in this `art/` folder under the file name given in its heading.
5. Skip any card whose image file already exists, so a run can be resumed or a single card regenerated by deleting its file.
6. Use `sunmane-lancer.png` as a reference for the painted style only, not for its pose, framing or sky.
7. Change only the files in this `art/` folder.
8. If you can't generate images in this environment, stop and say so. Don't substitute placeholders, drawings made in code, or images from the web.

When finished, list the files you created and any cards you couldn't complete.

## Style block

Apply this to every image:

```text
You are painting card art for an original high fantasy card game called Breachline.

Medium: traditional oil and acrylic fantasy illustration, with visible, confident brushwork and rich painted detail. It must never look like a photograph, 3D render, anime, comic or flat vector art.

Energy: lightly dynamic. The subject is doing something natural and believable, never just standing there and never melodramatic. Avoid extreme action poses, screaming faces, explosions of debris and exaggerated heroics.

Combat: if there is an enemy, it stays off-screen. Nothing attacks toward the viewer.

Background: implied, not fully realized. A single wall, doorway or stretch of ground suggests the wider place. Any other figures are few, secondary and much less detailed than the subject.

Power: the subject looks only as powerful as the prompt says. An ordinary person should look ordinary.

Variety: follow the facing and framing given in the prompt. Vary the skies and weather. Don't default to streaky diagonal brushstroke skies, rain, grey haze, or ivy on old stone unless the prompt asks for them.

Composition: landscape, about 3:2. One clear subject that reads at small size, with important details kept away from the edges.

Do not include: text, letters, card frames, borders, logos, watermarks, signatures, or characters and symbols from existing games, films or books.
```

## Lands

Land art has no characters.

### Ashen barrows: ashen-barrows.png

*Dominion land.*

Paint a long, low burial mound in the foreground, ringed with leaning pale standing stones, under a flat, cold sky at dusk. Fine grey ash lies over the dead grass. A second mound is only hinted at in the distance. The mood is still and quietly wrong. No people.

### Overgrown ruin: overgrown-ruin.png

*Genesis land.*

Paint a close view of a cracked marble floor, with one bright green sapling growing up through the widest crack in a shaft of soft daylight. The broken base of a great stone seat sits at the edge of the frame, mossy and half buried, which suggests a throne room without showing it. The mood is quiet and hopeful. No people.

## Vigil

### Water carrier: water-carrier.png

*Unit, cost 1. Heals an adjacent ally.*

Paint a thin white young man in a plain undyed tunic, shown full-body and moving left at a hurried, careful walk along a muddy track, with two wooden buckets on a shoulder yoke and water slopping over one rim. A small tin sun hangs at his neck. One bandaged arm reaches up from the bottom edge of the frame, barely detailed. He looks ordinary, tired and kind, with no armour or weapon. Soft morning light.

### Pride cub: pride-cub.png

*Unit, cost 1. Stronger next to an ally.*

Paint a lion folk adolescent with a scruffy, half-grown mane, wearing an ivory and gold breastplate slightly too large for them, shown from the knees up and turned to the left. They are mid-roar, more earnest than fearsome, holding a short spear too tightly. A much older lion folk warrior's armoured arm and hip stand at the right edge, out of focus. The cub is noble and young, never cartoonish. Clear blue sky.

### Oath sentinel: oath-sentinel.png

*Unit, cost 3. Protects adjacent allies.*

Paint a sturdy Black man in his fifties in worn ivory and steel armour, shown full-body and seen three-quarters from behind. He has planted a tall square shield with a gold sun on the ground in front of him, and looks back over his shoulder toward someone off-screen with calm reassurance. The top of a low stone wall runs behind him. He is steady and experienced, not a legend. Late afternoon gold light.

### Saint Maren of the Long Watch: saint-maren.png

*Unit, cost 6. A Velunn: a lesser divine being, on the scale of a saint. Heals all adjacent allies.*

Paint a very tall, weathered woman with long white hair and pale skin, in layered ivory and gold robes, walking slowly toward the viewer through wind-bent grass at dawn. Her open hands are held low at her sides, with soft gold light gathering around her fingers. Two soldiers kneel far behind her, barely detailed. She has quiet, immense presence and is plainly more than human, but there's no halo, wings or grand display. The sky is pale gold and nearly cloudless.

## Fervor

### Cinder Clan raiders: cinder-clan-raiders.png

*Unit, cost 2. Swift.*

Paint a wide shot of three human raiders in soot-stained leather and red sashes, vaulting a low wooden palisade from right to left. The lead is a white woman, laughing and looking back at the others as she lands. The other two are mid-climb and less detailed. They look like they're having the time of their lives. Bright, hard midday sun and a clear sky, with one thin line of smoke in the distance.

### Cinder berserker: cinder-berserker.png

*Unit, cost 3. Stronger when damaged.*

Paint a broad-shouldered white woman with a long copper braid, in scarred leather with iron bands, shown from the thighs up, striding to the left with a heavy axe held low in one hand. There's a fresh wound on her shoulder, which she hasn't noticed, and she's smiling slightly at something off-screen. Keep the face controlled, not screaming. Behind her is only dark rock lit by orange firelight from below.

### Mercenary captain: mercenary-captain.png

*Unit, cost 4. Directs an adjacent ally.*

Paint a Latino man in his forties in mismatched armour and a faded red sash, standing on the back of a supply cart, shown full-body from a slightly low angle. He's turned to the right, pointing with a rolled map and calling an order to someone off-screen. Two soldiers near the wheel are barely detailed. He looks competent and well liked, more of a working officer than a warlord. Overcast but bright sky.

### Ember drake: ember-drake.png

*Unit, cost 4. Flying.*

Paint a lean, horse-sized drake with soot-black scales and ember-orange wing membranes, banking to the left overhead, seen from below and to the side with its full body and wings in frame. Its head is turned, tracking something off-screen. It's scrappy and quick, clearly far smaller than a great dragon. The sky behind it is a clean orange dusk, with no land visible.

## Gnosis

### Illusory decoy: illusory-decoy.png

*Unit, cost 1. Enemies must attack it first.*

Paint a translucent, shimmering figure in a blue hooded cloak, standing full-body in a narrow lamplit alley with the wall visible through it. An arrow has passed cleanly through the centre of its chest and hangs in the air just behind it, trailing blue motes. The figure hasn't reacted at all, and its face is a smooth blur within the hood. Amber lamplight on dry cobblestones.

### Cloistered scholar: cloistered-scholar.png

*Unit, cost 2. Draws cards instead of moving or attacking.*

Paint an elderly white woman seated at a small desk in a narrow stone cell, seen side-on and facing left, with her whole figure in view. She's absorbed in an open book and turning a page, her face lit from below. A single candle burns among dozens of melted stubs covering the desk and floor, and books are stacked around her feet. One tiny high window shows pale daylight. This image can be still: the feeling is deep, content concentration.

### Lantern arcanist: lantern-arcanist.png

*Unit, cost 3. Ranged.*

Paint an East Asian man in his thirties in a deep blue coat with brass fastenings, seen from behind and to the right, standing at a stone parapet with one arm extended outward. A thin line of blue-white light leaves his fingertips and travels away toward an off-screen target in the far distance. A brass lantern sits on the parapet beside him. He's focused and unhurried. Clear dark blue night sky with stars.

### Brass chorister: brass-chorister.png

*Unit, a construct, cost 4. Pushes an adjacent enemy.*

Paint a tall, slim brass automaton from the Brass Choir, shown full-body and turned to the right, with a row of organ pipes rising from its shoulders and a round, open mouth like a horn. It's stepping forward with one riveted palm extended, and visible rings of pushed air leave the mouth and hand toward someone off-screen. Teal enamel details and small etched lantern stars decorate its plates. It looks like a beautifully made instrument, not a war machine. A workshop wall and warm lamplight sit behind it.

### Displacement: displacement.png

*Instant, cost 2. A unit dodges an attack by moving.*

Paint a Latina woman in a teal and brass mage's coat, shown full-body, stepping calmly away to the left and glancing back. Where she stood a moment ago, a fading blue afterimage of her is being cut through by the blade of a sword entering the frame from the right edge. Only the blade is visible, not its owner. Her expression is mildly curious. A plain stone wall sits behind her, under amber light.

### Binding glyph: binding-glyph.png

*Enchantment, cost 2. An enemy unit can't move.*

Paint a low view of an armoured soldier from the knees down on wet paving, one boot straining to lift. Thin rings of luminous blue script-like geometry circle both ankles and hold them to the ground, though none of it forms readable letters. The soldier's upper body is out of frame, and one dropped glove lies nearby. The feeling is puzzling and uncanny, not violent. Dim blue light.

## Dominion

### Gravebound thrall: gravebound-thrall.png

*Unit, cost 1.*

Paint a long-dead soldier in the rusted remains of armour, shown full-body and shuffling to the left across ash-covered ground, with a broken sword dragging behind it in one hand. Its head hangs low, with most of the face hidden by a dented helmet. There's dried skin and old bone but no gore. It looks pitiful more than terrifying. A leaning pale stone stands behind it under a flat grey sky.

### Bone harvester: bone-harvester.png

*Unit, cost 2. Destroys an ally to grow stronger.*

Paint a gaunt figure in layered grey robes, seen from behind and stooping to the right, with its hood up and face entirely hidden. It's gathering long bones from the ground into a wicker basket on its back, and it holds a hooked sickle of tarnished silver. It works patiently, like a farmer at harvest. Only low ash-covered ground and a violet evening sky surround it.

### Ashen Court noble: ashen-court-noble.png

*Unit, cost 3. Heals from the damage it deals.*

Paint a white woman in her fifties in immaculate ash grey and tarnished silver court dress, seated at the end of a long stone table, shown from the waist up and facing the viewer at a slight angle to the left. One gloved hand rests beside a silver cup, and she's listening with polite, patient attention. A kneeling figure in the near distance is barely detailed and out of focus. She's quietly frightening, with no fangs, heavy makeup or gothic costume. Cold window light from one side.

### Vashkar, Who Counts the Dead: vashkar.png

*Unit, cost 6. A Velunn: a lesser divine being, on the scale of a devil. Drains life.*

Paint a very tall, gaunt being with ash grey skin, long curved horns and fingers too long by a joint, seated on a low mound of old dented helmets, shown full-body and turned to the left. It's counting something on its fingers with absorbed, unhurried attention, and has a string of small knotted bones around its neck. It looks ancient and patient, plainly more than human but no ruler of hell, with no flames or wings. A flat violet dusk and bare ground surround it.

### Raise the dead: raise-the-dead.png

*Spell, cost 3. Returns a unit from the discard pile.*

Paint a fallen soldier in a muddy field, shown full-body, who has just sat up with their head hanging and their sword still in one limp hand. A gloved hand enters the frame from the top right, two fingers beckoning, with its owner out of view. Thin threads of violet light link the fingers to the soldier's chest. There's no gore. The mood is cold and matter-of-fact. Flat grey morning light.

### Death warrant: death-warrant.png

*Enchantment, cost 5. Destroys an enemy unit after a delay.*

Paint a soldier seen full-body from behind, walking away to the left along a quiet path in the evening and carrying a helmet under one arm, relaxed and unaware. On the back of their breastplate is a single handprint of black ash, faintly smoking. Their face isn't visible. A low wall and soft violet dusk sit beyond them. The image should feel peaceful until you notice the handprint.

## Genesis

### Walking sapling: walking-sapling.png

*Unit, cost 2. Grows over time.*

Paint a young tree the height of a child, walking on two thick root-feet across a mossy clearing, shown full-body and heading to the right. Its slim trunk has a simple suggestion of a face in the bark, and a few bright new leaves have just opened on its branches. It's gentle and curious. Dappled sunlight and a soft green background of implied trees.

### Thornwarden saboteur: thornwarden-saboteur.png

*Unit, cost 2. Recovers health over time.*

Paint a lean Black woman in bark-coloured leathers with a hood pushed back, crouching on top of a timber mill wheel and looking back over her shoulder to the left. She's threading a living, thorny vine through the spokes with gloved hands, and a small fresh cut on her forearm is already closing. Only the wet wood and a strip of river are visible. She's quick and sure of herself. Early morning light through mist.

### Great owl: great-owl.png

*Unit, cost 3. Flying.*

Paint an enormous mottled brown owl gliding silently left between dark tree trunks, seen side-on with its broad wings fully spread and every feather soft and detailed. Its head is turned to look just past the viewer with huge amber eyes. A single shaft of moonlight crosses it. Nothing else in the forest is sharply defined.

### Wayfinder druid: wayfinder-druid.png

*Unit, cost 3. Lets you play an extra land.*

Paint a wide shot of an old white man with a long grey beard and a plain moss-green cloak, kneeling to press one bare hand on the forest floor, seen three-quarters from behind and facing away to the right. Ahead of him, the undergrowth is parting into a faint new path, with small green shoots rising along it. A walking staff rests against his shoulder. He's humble and at ease. Warm late-day light through leaves.

### Elder Herd bull: elder-herd-bull.png

*Unit, cost 5. Very large. Excess damage carries through.*

Paint a colossal shaggy bull-like beast with moss growing along its back and huge, sweeping horns, walking steadily to the left in a side-on wide shot with its whole body in frame. Young trees bend and snap against its shoulder, and it doesn't appear to notice. A wide, clear sky over a grassy rise shows how massive it is. It's unhurried, not charging.

### Shifting forest: shifting-forest.png

*Instant, cost 1. Moves an enemy unit.*

Paint a forest path that simply stops: a worn dirt trail runs from the foreground into a solid wall of old trunks, with one thick root lying freshly across it and the soil still disturbed. A traveller's lantern, still lit, sits abandoned on the path. No people are visible. The light is dim and green, and the mood is unsettling but beautiful.
