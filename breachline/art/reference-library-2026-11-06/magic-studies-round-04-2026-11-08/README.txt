The creator praised the restrained treatment and approved both groups of three.
Use these as starting references and invent beyond their faces, kit, effects and silhouettes.
Visual approval does not define precise powers, costs, prevalence, faction ownership or drake taxonomy.

01 - Veil adept
01-veil-adept.png
A sorcerer folds shadow over part of herself. Tests an eerie, restrained spell expressed through darkness and disappearing form, with no equipment explaining it.

02 - Rime drake
02-rime-drake.png
A drake exhales a dense freezing breath. Tests creature magic and a cold visual language—condensation, rime and pale crystal—without apparatus or energy cords.

03 - Banked flame blade
03-banked-flame-blade.png
A plain fighting blade carries banked heat within the steel. Tests enchantment as a property of an item, with the bearer using it naturally and no added casting mechanism.

04 - Rain gathering
04-rain-gathering.png
Three people gather a brief, localized rain. Tests communal magic through a natural event, while leaving its source and ritual rules open.

05 - Enchanter's quench
05-enchanters-quench.png
A maker finishes an enchanted blade in an ordinary workshop. Tests a selective meeting of craft and magic; the enchantment's source stays unexplained.

06 - Stoneward colossus
06-stoneward-colossus.png
A monumental stone-and-bronze guardian has weight and presence but no displayed engine or glowing core. Tests a more mysterious large construct alongside the mechanical crane.
