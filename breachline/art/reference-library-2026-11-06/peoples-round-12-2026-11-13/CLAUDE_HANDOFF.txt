BREACHLINE — PEOPLES ROUND 12 FINAL SELECTION
Creator decision — 2026-11-13

“accept river folk and saltreach, reject all else”

ACCEPTED DIRECTION
Riverfolk: retain the existing fin ears, neck gills, subdued scales and ordinary human body proportions. Use the approved waterworks, small-settlement and domestic scenes; R5 and R6 remain especially liked. R1v2's quieter workers and R3v2's corrected water are now approved.
Saltreach: a human coastal culture with rugged, mildly sunburnt, weathered faces, oiled wool and practical sea gear. The weathered sheet and boat-hauling scene are accepted.

REJECTED
Scions, Graftkin and the two new made-people studies, Glimmerfolk, Mourncaps, Lichenkin and Cairnfold. This final choice supersedes earlier praise or revision requests for these round-12 concepts. Their images are removed; do not implement or restore them from the historical briefs. Unrelated earlier people references retain their existing status.

IMPLEMENTATION SCOPE
The nine images below are accepted visual references and scene concepts. No cards are selected by this review, and visual approval does not fix biological capabilities, universal professions, prevalence or game rules. Carry the accepted Riverfolk and Saltreach direction into the next setting pass while preserving existing setting constraints.
