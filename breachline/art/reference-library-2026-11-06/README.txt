REFERENCE USE - STARTING POINTS FOR FURTHER DESIGN
These sheets establish useful starting examples of Breachline's anatomy,
painted treatment, materials and believable construction. The creator wants
the visual repertoire to keep expanding: new faces, body builds, clothing,
armor, weapons and combinations are welcome beyond everything shown here.
A listed face, outfit or weapon is optional. A new design does not need a
direct counterpart in this collection.

For new characters, carry forward species-defining anatomy and the art
direction while inventing an individual appearance and belongings. Preserve
a particular face or exact kit when depicting that same established character
or when the brief explicitly requests it. A face sheet is not the entire cast.

Explore variation through silhouette, cut, proportion, material, craft,
condition, role and personal taste. Include well-maintained and modest gear
as well as worn pieces. Simple construction can support considerable variety;
variation does not require extra layers, straps or ornament.
Review new batches alongside recent art for repeated faces, silhouettes,
outfits and weapons. Changing only color, wear or a small accessory is not
enough when the underlying design keeps recurring.

Use the setting and latest creator feedback to maintain coherence. A sample
character's job, clothes or belongings do not define their whole people or
faction. The library should help new work belong in the world while allowing
it to go beyond these initial examples.

CURRENT DIRECTION — CONFIRMED 2026-11-09
Canonical source: ../../../.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md
These defaults apply to new card, character, equipment, creature and world art.
Interface paintings use the same material principles with quieter contrast.

CASTING: For new highly aggressive frontline fighters, the vast majority should be men; female soldiers remain occasional. Women remain welcome as mages, archers, civilians and other characters. Preserve specifically approved identities unless the creator requests a change.

FACES: Actively consult the human/species face sheets. Use specific ordinary reference faces for corrective casting, and draw on their varied skulls, noses, jaws, ages and asymmetries when inventing new people. Keep generally appealing faces and occasional exceptional beauty; avoid repeated model faces, compulsory ugliness and uncanny skin detail.

MATERIALS: Skin, wool, wood and stone have naturally diffuse surfaces. Reserve stronger reflections for appropriate metal, glass or water. Avoid waxy faces, uniform surface shine and habitual red cheeks, especially on heavyset women. Keep vibrant color and convincing light.

PAINTED ATTENTION: Match brush size, edge precision and detail locally. Focal features can be precise; secondary people and distant places stay legible with fewer marks and quieter contrast. Do not combine coarse texture with uniformly hairline detail, render everything equally sharply, or substitute camera blur and tunnel focus.

DESIGN RANGE: Constructs may have abstract functional anatomy, asymmetry and open space; they need not resemble armored human knights. Gnosis combines scholarly high fantasy with broader steam-era craft, not an optics uniform. Blend selected Asian armor details into the established kit; modest curved eaves are a valid architectural option. These are examples, not mandatory formulas.

MAGIC AND PRESENTATION: Direct personal and creature magic, imbued objects and industrial applications can coexist without universal equipment, visible power feeds or glowing threads. Keep grounded steam machinery and creature/direct magical flight distinct from the rejected hovering magitech vessels. Choose a compelling image; card rules do not require an ability demonstration.

REFERENCE USE: Accepted references guide anatomy, materials, individual continuity and taste; they do not limit new faces, clothes, kit, settings or magic to a repeating catalog. Preserve accepted content and vary presentation. Feedback applies at the image or batch scope given unless the creator makes it general.

LATEST MAGIC BALANCE CLARIFICATION - 2026-11-08
Magic, physical items and industry were too tightly intertwined in the later
experiments. Equipment is not required by default, nor is a visible caster
directly feeding every effect or machine. People and creatures can wield magic;
items can also hold magical energy that users harness. Keep the range open.
Do not default to gauntlet augmentations or thread/ribbon effects. The accepted
field loom, censer-healer and cosmological strand remain specific valid examples.
Mechanical/magitech hovering boats are rejected. Prefer creatures or explicit
flight magic for aerial travel, with exact capabilities still open.
Explore broad limits across scales and alignments before locking faction
approaches. The steam walking-crane revision and rolling mill remain pending;
none of the rejected scale studies is added to this library.

USE IN FUTURE ART
1. Begin with the new character, role, action and scene the illustration needs.
2. Consult only the sheets useful to that brief, identifying what each teaches:
   species anatomy, painted treatment, material, construction or silhouette.
3. For a new character, invent a distinct face, build and personal equipment.
   Adapt, combine or depart from examples as the role warrants. Unlisted
   clothing and weapon designs are welcome within the setting and art direction.
4. For the SAME established character, preserve the required identity and any
   specified signature equipment. Do not automatically copy their entire outfit.
5. Design the pose, camera, light and environment for the new scene. Reference
   sheet poses, neutral clothing and old painting backgrounds are not defaults.
6. Keep functional construction and fit, including species-specific needs such
   as mane, tusk or hoof clearance. Favor clear design over decorative clutter.
7. Compare with recent artwork: vary faces, silhouettes, cuts, materials and
   equipment choices. Avoid repeating the catalog or rotating through its kits.
8. Treat new visual explorations as proposals; existing lore and creator
   decisions still govern the world.

REUSABLE PROMPT STARTER - NEW DESIGN
Use the selected Breachline sheets as initial references for [species anatomy,
painted treatment, material or construction]. Develop a NEW individual and
equipment appropriate to [role, context and scene]. Explore a distinct face,
silhouette, clothing cut and practical gear; designs absent from the sheets
are welcome. Keep established species traits, coherent materials and clear
construction. The examples are not a fixed cast, wardrobe or equipment list.
Choose the presentation for [visual aim, subject and scene]; action is optional.
Apply the current casting, faces, material and painted-attention defaults above.
Use fresh staging, camera and lighting. Review against recent art to avoid
repeating the same faces, outfits, weapons and compositions.

OPTIONAL CONTINUITY ADDENDUM - SAME CHARACTER
This image depicts [established character]. Preserve [specified identity cues
and any required signature equipment] from [reference]. Adapt everything else
to this scene and role as appropriate. Exact reuse is a continuity choice,
not the default for new characters.

STATUS AND DESIGN NOTES
These are painted concept turnarounds, not measured technical orthographics.
Equal display size across sheets does not imply equal in-world height.
Neutral clothes are a reference convenience, not a universal culture.
The images have off-white painted backgrounds, not transparency.
Orcs retain coarse nonhuman facial structure, including female castings.
Lion folk keep feline skulls; goblins keep their distinct ears and muzzles.
Alderkin have unadorned botanical anatomy rather than human clothes.
Stonehorn remains exploratory. Species status otherwise follows SETTING.md.
Constructs, Velunn, vampires and undead are not added as biological races.
Opaline Kin remain excluded. The original face studies and equipment sheets
14-17 remain proposals; first-batch Vigil and Gnosis equipment is retained.
The eight sheets in character-kits-2026-11-08/ are accepted visual references.
Nothing here changes the gameplay files or replaces existing card paintings.
