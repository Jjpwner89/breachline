BREACHLINE - ACCEPTED CHARACTER AND KIT REFERENCES
2026-11-08 | 8 selected sheets | Built-in image generation

These are starting examples for further invention, not a fixed cast or a universal faction kit.
Keep creating new faces, armor, clothing, weapons and combinations. Preserve exact identity only for continuity or an explicit request.
Visual approval does not automatically make proposed magical functions, material performance or cultural traditions canon.
The blue Gnosis breastplate is a specific positive reference for high-medieval craftsmanship.
The mirror device was rejected and deleted. Sheet 05 has the requested male foot soldier.

01 - Oath-road veteran
01-oath-road-veteran.png
Context: Human; Vigil
Intention: A practical middle rank between militia and gilded champion: maintained brigandine, useful helmet, narrow shield and a veteran face. The sun is a small personal allegiance cue.
Kit: Ivory brigandine; Kettle helmet; Narrow shield; Arming sword.

02 - Orc saddle-raider

Context: Orc; Fervor
Intention: An independently equipped raider built for mobility: quilted vest, short mail mantle, compact bow and chopping blade. This tests personal, mixed-source belongings rather than organized red uniforms.
Kit: Quilted vest and mail mantle; Short recurve bow; Chopping blade and sheath; Open-brow helmet.

07 - Bronze-harness alderkin

Context: Alderkin; Unassigned; Genesis-adjacent possibility
Intention: An alderkin choosing forged protection and a spear for its own purposes. Tests metal equipment fitted to living tree anatomy, with broad padded supports and no nails through bark. No automatic allegiance or duty to humans.

08 - Clayplate giant
