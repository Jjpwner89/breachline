BREACHLINE - ACCEPTED MAGIC AND CRAFT REFERENCES
2026-11-08 | Five accepted sheets

These are starting references, not a closed cast, equipment list or universal magic system.
The loom and wardwright are positive examples of enchantment and craft. They do not require all magic to use equipment.
People and creatures can wield magic; a visible direct caster is also not a universal requirement.
Avoid repeating thread-like effects outside contexts that warrant them. Preserve these specific approvals.

01 - Harmonic wardwright
01-harmonic-wardwright.png
Creator: Strongly liked: a simple addition visibly increases the character's capability.
Original intention: A scholar uses a stringed casting gauntlet to hold broken stone in a precise pattern. Tests armored spellcraft and resonance instruments while carrying forward the well-built blue breastplate.

02 - Spellwright's field loom
02-spellwright-field-loom.png
Creator: Liked: industry and magic combined in a way that fits the alignment.
Original intention: A portable loom draws a temporary ward out of light. Tests scholarly magic stored and shaped through a handmade mechanism, with the spell doing the extraordinary work.

06 - Still Flame censer-healer
06-still-flame-censer-healer.png
Creator: Healer liked.
Original intention: A traveling healer draws a gentle, luminous bandage from a small censer. Tests tangible healing magic and portable ritual objects; it does not prove the gods are answering.

07 - Cinder-cant drummer
07-cinder-cant-drummer.png
Creator: Drummer liked.
Original intention: A goblin channels heat and resolve through a battle rhythm. Tests communal, exuberant magic with personal kit, rather than scholarly apparatus or an organized army uniform.

08 - Stormhook channeler
08-stormhook-channeler.png
Creator: Storm magic liked.
Original intention: An orc catches unstable storm force on a rough iron hook-spear. Tests Fervor beyond fire: forceful, risky elemental magic shaped through nerve and a simple tool.
