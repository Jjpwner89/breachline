# Breachline art briefs, set 16: cards for the new mechanics

Workflow: [briefs to paintings](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/BRIEF_WORKFLOW.md). Art direction: [art direction](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md).

- **Date and revision:** 2026-11-14, revision 1.
- **Source:** new cards built on the mechanics the creator liked on 2026-11-14 (Seasoned, Grace, Stratagem, Cadence, Recall, Ransack, Offering, Reap, Surge, Empower, Momentum, Hired, Deep roots). Each statline passed a paired balance check with the lookahead bot, and Sonnet playtesters played the mechanics in ten full games. The card table at the end is the design record for these briefs.
- **Card status:** proposals. The creator has not yet accepted or rejected these cards; names, subjects and the four animals' card roles are proposals too. The creator asked for briefs to be ready so painting can start while they review. A painting for a card the creator later cuts is simply unused.
- **Scope:** 22 finished card paintings (M1–M22). No lineups are needed: every person and animal here uses an accepted design.
- **Output stage:** finished card paintings at 1536 × 1024, landscape.
- **WIP folder:** `art/work-in-progress/set16-mechanic-cards-2026-11-14/`. Proposed filenames are `<card id>.png`.
- **Paths:** relative to `breachline/`.
- **Status:** ready for painting.

## Applies to the whole batch

- Variety matters across these 22: eight peoples and animals, indoor and outdoor, day, night and weather, close and wide.
- Four are stratagems: a card set face down that springs on an enemy's action. Paint the place or the people waiting, not the trap going off, unless that makes the stronger picture.
- Faction emblems only where they read and make sense (creator, Set 15).

## M1 / oathveteran — Oath veteran
Type / alignment / faction: unit / Vigil / Sunmane Oath. Output: `oathveteran.png`.

Subject and setting: an older Sunmane Oath soldier, well past the age most stop fighting, still in the line. Human; any build.

What makes it worth painting: experience written on a person, not on armour. Kit that has been repaired many times and fits him exactly. A quiet moment, such as checking a strap before a march, or simply standing easy among younger soldiers, works as well as a fight.

Must keep: Sunmane Oath kit (plate cuirass, helmet, shield, sword), worn and personal rather than parade-ready. The sun on the shield or tabard, once.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/09-vigil-equipment.png`, top row (required: Sunmane kit). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/people/01-humans.png` and `08-humans-additional-faces.png` (optional: faces).

Design context — not for the image prompt: common 3-mana 2/4 with Seasoned, growing each time it destroys a unit. See the card table.

## M2 / flameduelist — Still Flame duelist
Type / alignment / faction: unit / Vigil / Still Flame. Output: `flameduelist.png`.

Subject and setting: a Greyhide who fights for the Still Flame. Greyhides value healing above every other craft and are rarely angry, so a Greyhide in a fight is unusual and serious. A cold northern coast, a longhouse or a tide road is home ground.

What makes it worth painting: a large, kind person who has chosen to fight. Calm weight and gentle hands holding a weapon. The Still Flame's warmth can be present in light or a small ritual detail; it needn't glow.

Must keep: the final Greyhide design (rounded body, smooth grey hide, broad calm face, short sturdy legs, fine hands, no tail). Cold-coast clothing.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/peoples-final-2026-11-13/07-greyhides-final.png` (required: identity). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/magic-and-craft-2026-11-08/06-still-flame-censer-healer.png` (optional: Still Flame kit and censer).

Design context — not for the image prompt: uncommon 3-mana 2/3 with Drain and Grace (healing beyond full health becomes a ward). Don't show a ward bubble or a healing demonstration.

## M3 / heldspears — Held spears
Type / alignment / faction: stratagem / Vigil / Sunmane Oath. Output: `heldspears.png`.

Subject and setting: a line of Sunmane Oath spearmen lying in wait, in tall grass, a sunken lane or behind a field wall, spears low.

What makes it worth painting: stillness before something happens. Held breath, a hand signal, the one soldier who looks up. Dawn or dusk light through grass could carry it.

Must keep: Sunmane kit, practical and field-worn. Ordinary soldiers, not champions.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/09-vigil-equipment.png` (required: kit).

Design context — not for the image prompt: a 2-mana stratagem; when an enemy attacks your unit or summoner, the attacker takes 3 damage first.

## M4 / secondworking — Second working
Type / alignment / faction: spell / Gnosis / Lantern Conclave. Output: `secondworking.png`.

Subject and setting: a Glimmerfolk mage of the Lantern Conclave casting a second spell straight on the heels of the first. Their glimmers flare when they cast, so their magic can't be hidden. The undercity of Ambrel is a natural home; a Conclave hall or street works too.

What makes it worth painting: the glimmers themselves blazing along the arms into the hands, one working still fading as the next begins. Concentration, not spectacle.

Must keep: the final Glimmerfolk design (slender body, long neck, broad ear-fans, blue luminous freckles on matte dark skin; dotted arm lines denser toward the hands; eyes don't glow). No sound magic.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/peoples-final-2026-11-13/02-glimmerfolk-final.png` (required: identity). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/10-gnosis-equipment.png` (optional: Conclave dress).

Design context — not for the image prompt: 2-mana spell, 3 damage, cheaper after another spell that turn (Cadence).

## M5 / senthome — Sent home
Type / alignment / faction: spell / Gnosis / Lantern Conclave. Output: `senthome.png`.

Subject and setting: a summoned creature, or a conjured warrior, coming undone and returning to where it came from, while a Conclave mage closes the working.

What makes it worth painting: something large being folded away by something precise. The unmaking can be strange and quiet rather than violent.

Must keep: Gnosis magic is learned and practical. Portals are rare, so show an unmaking, not a doorway.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/10-gnosis-equipment.png` (optional: Conclave dress).

Design context — not for the image prompt: 1-mana spell; return a unit to its owner's hand, and its enchantments fall off (Recall).

## M8 / writseizure — Writ of seizure
Type / alignment / faction: spell / Dominion / Ashen Court. Output: `writseizure.png`.

Subject and setting: Ashen Court officers emptying a household under a sealed writ, while the family watches. Dominion life is harsh; this is ordinary for them.

What makes it worth painting: the imbalance between the paper and the people. One official reading, men carrying out a chest, a mother holding something back. The Price of Morning's oppressive ordinary life is the right feeling.

Must keep: Ashen Court, never benevolent. The household is poor, and local.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/17-dominion-personal-kits.png` (optional: Ashen Court dress).

Design context — not for the image prompt: 3-mana spell; the enemy discards 2 random cards (Ransack 2).

## M10 / gatheredhost — Gathered host
Type / alignment / faction: unit / Dominion / Gravebound. Output: `gatheredhost.png`.

Subject and setting: a huge Gravebound thing made of many dead bound into one body, raised from what its makers gave up. The Gravebound want the slide into ruin to finish.

What makes it worth painting: scale and wrongness without gore: a silhouette that reads as one creature until you see the many. Keep it distinct from the existing Barrow colossus.

Must keep: human-sized familiar objects keep a consistent scale. Not an Ashborn; the Ashborn are fully repaired individuals.

References: none required.

Design context — not for the image prompt: rare 6-mana 4/5 with Offering (destroy one of your own units as you summon it to make it cheaper).
