## Applies to the whole batch

- Variety matters across these 22: eight peoples and animals, indoor and outdoor, day, night and weather, close and wide.
- Four are stratagems: a card set face down that springs on an enemy's action. Paint the place or the people waiting, not the trap going off, unless that makes the stronger picture.
- Faction emblems only where they read and make sense (creator, Set 15).

## M1 / oathveteran — Oath veteran
Type / alignment / faction: unit / Vigil / Sunmane Oath. Output: `oathveteran.png`.

Subject and setting: an older Sunmane Oath soldier, well past the age most stop fighting, still in the line. Human; any build.

What makes it worth painting: experience written on a person, not on armour. Kit that has been repaired many times and fits him exactly. A quiet moment, such as checking a strap before a march, or simply standing easy among younger soldiers, works as well as a fight.

Must keep: Sunmane Oath kit (plate cuirass, helmet, shield, sword), worn and personal rather than parade-ready. The sun on the shield or tabard, once.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/09-vigil-equipment.png`, top row (required: Sunmane kit). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/people/01-humans.png` and `08-humans-additional-faces.png` (optional: faces).

Design context — not for the image prompt: common 3-mana 2/4 with Seasoned, growing each time it destroys a unit. See the card table.

## M3 / heldspears — Held spears
Type / alignment / faction: stratagem / Vigil / Sunmane Oath. Output: `heldspears.png`.

Subject and setting: a line of Sunmane Oath spearmen lying in wait, in tall grass, a sunken lane or behind a field wall, spears low.

What makes it worth painting: stillness before something happens. Held breath, a hand signal, the one soldier who looks up. Dawn or dusk light through grass could carry it.

## M4 / secondworking — Second working
Type / alignment / faction: spell / Gnosis / Lantern Conclave. Output: `secondworking.png`.

Subject and setting: a Glimmerfolk mage of the Lantern Conclave casting a second spell straight on the heels of the first. Their glimmers flare when they cast, so their magic can't be hidden. The undercity of Ambrel is a natural home; a Conclave hall or street works too.

What makes it worth painting: the glimmers themselves blazing along the arms into the hands, one working still fading as the next begins. Concentration, not spectacle.

Must keep: the final Glimmerfolk design (slender body, long neck, broad ear-fans, blue luminous freckles on matte dark skin; dotted arm lines denser toward the hands; eyes don't glow). No sound magic.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/peoples-final-2026-11-13/02-glimmerfolk-final.png` (required: identity). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/10-gnosis-equipment.png` (optional: Conclave dress).

Design context — not for the image prompt: 2-mana spell, 3 damage, cheaper after another spell that turn (Cadence).

## M21 / saltmigrant — Talloch
Type / alignment / faction: unit / Genesis / Elder Herd. Revision 8. Output: `saltmigrant.png` (the card id stays saltmigrant so saved decks keep working).

Subject and setting: the Talloch, a huge feathered heavyweight, genuinely strong Genesis animal. Its home range isn't set, so choose an open landscape that suits the painting without making it a habitat decision.

What makes it worth painting: contained power. Show its height, weight and presence, calm but able to do a great deal if roused. Awe rather than horror. A clear sense of scale, from a person, a tree or the land around it, helps.

Must keep: the selected M21-23 v5 reference: raised neck and crest, slate, cream and ochre feathers, a compact closed muzzle, powerful articulated legs and broad furred forepaws with stout, gently curved bear-like claws. No talons, and never the earlier dangling mouth. Not Highneck or the mane-backed alternative.

References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/set16-selected-2026-11-15/m21-23-v5-feathered-heavyweight.png` (required: anatomy, feathers, claws and colour; the person shows scale only). `m21-23-v2-leg-support.png` in the same folder (optional: leg build).
