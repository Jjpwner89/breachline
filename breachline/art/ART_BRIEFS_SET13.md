# Breachline art briefs, set 13: creature cards and wildlife in existing scenes

Workflow: [briefs to paintings](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/BRIEF_WORKFLOW.md). Art direction: [art direction](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md) and, for animals, [Breachline animal art](../../.claude/skills/breachline-animal-art/SKILL.md) with the [animal reference guide](../ANIMAL_REFERENCE_HANDOFF.txt) and [reference index](accepted-animals-2026-11-13/animal-reference-index.json).

- **Date and revision:** 2026-11-14, revision 1.
- **Source:** the creator chose these animals for cards and scene additions on 2026-11-14. Card designs are rows #216–224 in [CARD_PROPOSALS.md](../CARD_PROPOSALS.md).
- **Scope:** nine new card paintings (one starts with a harness lineup), and six edits that add wildlife to approved card paintings. The Ashborn kennel-hand is not in this batch; it waits on who makes the Copper hounds.
- **Output stage:** finished card paintings at 1536 × 1024, except S13-06, which starts with a lineup.
- **WIP folder:** `art/work-in-progress/set13-creature-cards-2026-11-14/`.
- **Paths:** relative to `breachline/`. A "cell" is a panel of a 3 × 2 sheet, numbered 1–3 across the top row and 4–6 across the bottom.
- **Status:** all items ready, none generated.

These animals are selected world references. Keep their anatomy, faces and coverings; their open details (diet, range, life cycle) stay open, so a painting's setting is a choice for that picture, not a settled habitat. Most animals here simply live. None of them is livestock unless a brief says so.

## New card paintings

## S13-01 / vaultstalker — Vaultstalker
Type / alignment: unit / Genesis / wild animal
Source and status: card #216; LG1-4; ready
Output: card painting, `vaultstalker.png`

Subject and setting:
A scaled predator the size of a large deer, about 2 m long, that waits in broken rocky ground. The creator called it a larger honey-badger impression with scales.

What makes it worth painting:
Weight held low and still among boulders and scree, the shoulder arch reading against the sky or a pale rock face. Watchful, not snarling.

Must keep:
The selected head, shoulder arch and low rear body; its scale covering.

References:
`art/work-in-progress/larger-creatures-2026-11-13/01-larger-wildlife.png`, cell 4 (required: identity and anatomy).

Design context — not for the image prompt:
Genesis uncommon; arrives behind the lines (Ambush). See #216.

## S13-04 / woodbreaker — Woodland breaker

Output: card painting, `woodland-breaker.png`

An extra-large, beaked, clawed forager that opens deadwood in old woodland. What it digs out of the logs is still undeveloped.

## S13-05 / fanback — Fanback
Type / alignment: unit / Genesis / wild animal (legendary)
Source and status: card #220; MG6; ready
Output: card painting, `fanback.png`

Subject and setting:
A massive animal whose thin, high body is carried on long jointed legs beneath a row of translucent back fans. Habitat, diet, fan function and the hanging strands are all unresolved.

What makes it worth painting:
Rare and awe-inspiring, not frightening. Light coming through the fans and the slow stride of something enormous across wide country. A small far-off figure or landmark can give the scale. The landscape is a choice for this painting, not a settled range.

Must keep:
The MN2-1 silhouette and coverings as selected. No threatening mouth or horror cues.

References:
`art/reference-library-2026-11-06/final-animal-selections-2026-11-13/mn2-freer-giants.png`, cell 1 (required: identity). Ignore the other cells.

## S13-06 / veilwing — Veilwing, harnessed
Type / alignment: unit / Gnosis / working animal (creator direction, 2026-11-14)
Source and status: card #221; MN2-5, chosen by the creator on 2026-11-14 from a set-aside study; ready for a lineup
Output: first, a harness lineup of about six takes, `veilwing-harness-lineup.png`; then, after the creator picks, the card painting `veilwing.png`

Subject and setting:
A huge winged animal with broad translucent sails and a slender trailing body. The creator wants it to be Gnosis and to wear a harness. This is the first giant shown working with people. Flight mechanism, diet and range remain open. Proposed purpose: the Lantern Conclave's survey flights, with instruments, observers or charting gear slung beneath. Aerial travel by creature fits the setting; there are no flying machines.

What makes it worth painting:
The lineup should vary how a harness can sit on that slender body without spoiling its line: rigging, slung cradles, light frames, how people reach it. The painting can then give the scale and grace of a survey flight, with the riders tiny against the sails.

Must keep:
The MN2-5 sail shapes, translucency and trailing body. It remains awe-inspiring, not a beast of burden or a war mount.

References:
`art/reference-library-2026-11-06/final-animal-selections-2026-11-13/mn2-freer-giants.png`, cell 5 (required: identity). Ignore the other cells.

Design context — not for the image prompt:
Gnosis rare flier (Flying). See #221. Record the selection of MN2-5 in the animal guide and index when the lineup is reviewed.

## S13-09 / specimenkeeper — Riverfolk specimen-keeper
Type / alignment: unit / Gnosis / Riverfolk
Source and status: card #224; ER5; ready
Output: card painting, `riverfolk-specimen-keeper.png`

Subject and setting:
A Riverfolk hydrologist in Ambrel's waterworks or at a canal edge, keeping Ford-threads in glass jars for study. Riverfolk do hydrology and charting for Gnosis, usually from a less privileged position. The Ford-threads are studied, not pets, and whether they live in fresh or salt water is still open.

What makes it worth painting:
Light passing through the jars and the translucent animals inside, and the keeper's absorbed attention.

Must keep:
Riverfolk anatomy: human proportions, fin-like ears, gill folds on the neck, patches of scale. The Ford-thread's selected body.

References:
`art/reference-library-2026-11-06/great-animal-selections-2026-11-13/er5.png` (required: Ford-thread). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/peoples-round-12-2026-11-13/01-r1-morning-shift-v2.png` and `art/eel-netter.png` (required: Riverfolk continuity).

Design context — not for the image prompt:
Gnosis common unit with the Study ability. See #224.
