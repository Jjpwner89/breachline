# Breachline art briefs, set 14: wildlife on the campaign maps and basic lands

Workflow: [briefs to paintings](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/BRIEF_WORKFLOW.md). Art direction: [art direction](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md) and, for animals, [Breachline animal art](../../.claude/skills/breachline-animal-art/SKILL.md) with the [animal reference guide](../ANIMAL_REFERENCE_HANDOFF.txt) and [reference index](accepted-animals-2026-11-13/animal-reference-index.json). Campaign maps: [map briefs](CAMPAIGN_MAP_BRIEFS.md).

- **Date and revision:** 2026-11-14, revision 1.
- **Source:** the creator wants this world's own wildlife to show up quietly in places players already look: the campaign maps and the basic land cards. It needn't be everywhere or obvious.
- **Scope:** edits to four approved campaign maps and three approved land paintings. Nothing new is painted from scratch. The Dominion map stays as it is: its emptiness suits it.
- **Output stage:** edited paintings at the original size and filename, with the previous version kept as history.
- **WIP folder:** `art/work-in-progress/set14-wildlife-maps-and-lands-2026-11-14/`.
- **Paths:** relative to `breachline/`. A "cell" is a panel of a 3 × 2 sheet, numbered 1–3 across the top row and 4–6 across the bottom.
- **Status:** all items ready, none generated. S14-M2's Veilwing waits on the harness picked in [set 13](ART_BRIEFS_SET13.md), S13-06.

## Rules for every item

- Use the approved painting as the edit source. Change only what an item names, and keep its light, palette and brushwork.
- Animals live their own lives here. They aren't livestock unless an item says so, and a painting's placement doesn't settle an animal's range.
- Keep each animal's selected anatomy, read at the scale it's shown.

**Maps only:** the game draws route markers and labels over each map at fixed positions, listed per map as percentages across and down. Keep every landmark, road, building and river where it is, and keep animals well clear of those positions. At map scale the animals are small, like the existing figures and boats: recognisable from above by silhouette, with a shadow where that helps, never a featured close-up.

## Campaign maps

## S14-M1 / map-genesis — The Green Return
Source: `art/maps/map-genesis.png`. Route markers: mother grove 84, 16; reedways 58, 24; cut line 39, 41; dam 48, 76; green city 16, 72.

The map shows Earth animals: elephants crossing the river at the ford (right of centre) and deer grazing in the marsh (upper middle). Replace them with this world's animals:
- **Elephants → Elder Herd bulls**, keeping the same small herd and the people walking alongside. The lore codex already describes herd-folk who walk with the great beasts. Reference: `art/elder-herd-bull.png` (L15; required).
- **Deer → Gravelmouths**, large lake-plant grazers standing in the marsh shallows. Keep them clear of the reedways marker. Reference: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/great-animal-selections-2026-11-13/g2.png`, cell 3 (G2-3; required).
- **Optional:** one Fanback far off at a forest edge, small and easy to miss. Giants are rare and awe-inspiring, never threatening. Reference: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/final-animal-selections-2026-11-13/mn2-freer-giants.png`, cell 1 (MG6).
