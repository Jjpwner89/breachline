BREACHLINE — PEOPLES IN THE WORLD
13 NOVEMBER 2026
Scenes first; card art second. All new scenes, names, jobs, places and card designs are proposals. No live-game cards have been added. Costs and rules are untested drafts.

SCENE PASS

S1 — Oren at the Broken Span
Kitefolk | Vigil | Unaffiliated
A veteran courier waits for the gust that will carry him across a broken mountain bridge. His practical vigilance proposes a place for Kitefolk in dangerous travel without making all of them messengers.

S2 — Sera in the Lamplit Court
Glimmerfolk | Gnosis | Unaffiliated
Sera repairs a household light in a deep-set courtyard while evening traffic passes above. Learned magic appears as skilled work; this is one artisan’s life, not a fixed occupation for Glimmerfolk.

S3 — Hessa’s Winter Door
Moor folk | Genesis | Unaffiliated
Hessa repairs a bow at the threshold of a moorland household as rain sweeps over the marsh. The scene gives the Moor folk domestic life and an individual defender, without making their society merely a service to humans.

CONTINUITY AND SCOPE
Final anatomy: Kitefolk KR1, Glimmerfolk GR1, Moor folk PL7, made people PL3D.
PL3D is the accepted original before the rejected stronger scars. Made people are Dominion; their precise origin and weak/absent soul remain unresolved.
Salt–silt folk: one species/two variants; PL8B still awaiting visual review. Crest folk: selected PL9.4 identity with proposed CR3 body crests. Coast folk: selected PL10.1 base in CO1.
The creator explicitly requested scenes then card art using these characters; pending visual exploration is not automatically finalized by its use here.
All new faction links are proposals. Most people in this catalogue have no faction affiliation.
Draft rules follow the existing broad game vocabulary; they have not been implemented or simulated. Movement, return, timing and duration details should be resolved with the creator before implementation.

PRODUCTION
Generated with the built-in image tool, one painting per request. Exact prompts, reference paths and file hashes are in prompts-and-manifest.json.
The catalogue keeps scene paintings separate from card paintings. Artwork has no baked-in frame, costs, rules or text.
