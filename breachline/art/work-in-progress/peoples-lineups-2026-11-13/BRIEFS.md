# Peoples lineups (2026-11-13)

This round retries two nonhuman peoples (Glimmerfolk and the made people) and adds a sheet of brand-new nonhuman peoples. It's the first step of the new workflow: **lineup → reference → scene**.

Each brief is one lineup sheet showing six quite different takes on one people. The creator points at the take that works, that one becomes a reference sheet, and only then does it get scenes. Lineups are for range: six takes that are too similar waste the sheet.

## Instructions for the image pass

1. Before writing any prompt, read:
   - [the art direction](../../../../.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md)
   - the [setting](../../../SETTING.md)

   The art direction's current rules win over this brief. Its HISTORY.md is background only.
2. Make four 1536 × 1024 landscape PNGs in this folder, named in each heading.

   **Mix retries with new variety.** In each retry sheet:
   - Takes 1–4 develop what was liked or nearly worked.
   - Takes 5–6 are fresh departures that keep only the people's core idea.
   - The made-people sheet has its own six directions, with take 6 as the open one.
   - Sheet 4 is entirely new peoples.
3. **Lineup sheet format:**
   - Warm ivory, empty background.
   - Two rows of three cells.
   - Each cell holds one adult full body, head to feet visible, standing in a relaxed neutral pose, with a larger head-and-shoulders study of the same individual beside it.
   - All six at the same scale, so height differences read.
   - Plain neutral clothing, so anatomy carries the differences. The made people may wear plain dark Dominion clothing.
   - No text, numbers, labels or frames in the image. The gallery labels positions 1–6 in reading order (top row left to right, then bottom row).
4. **Painting:**
   - Painted reference-sheet finish: matte skin, coherent light and shadow shapes, diffuse daylight.
   - No camera blur. The blur experiment applies to scenes only, not sheets.
   - Varied faces drawn from the human sheets for proportion and individuality, not copied.
5. **Attachments:**
   - Attach only the human face sheets, for face variety:
     - `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/active-scenes-and-people-2026-11-11/01-human-faces-songweaver-and-new-neighbors.png`
     - `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/active-scenes-and-people-2026-11-11/02-human-faces-cloud-silk-and-new-neighbors.png`
     - `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/active-scenes-and-people-2026-11-11/03-human-faces-white-men.png`
   - Don't attach the Riverfolk sheet or any finished painting, or the model will copy them.
6. **Records:**
   - Record prompts in `prompts-and-manifest.json`.
   - Build a `review.html` showing each sheet with numbered positions 1–6.
   - Beside each sheet, give one line per take and a two-to-three-sentence write-up of the sheet.
7. Write only this folder.
8. **The defining trait reads at a glance.** Understatement is for magic, not for what makes a people look like itself. Check hands, limb counts and joins before showing.

## PL2 · 02-glimmerfolk-lineup

**The people:**
- Tunnel-dwelling people with large eyes and freckles that glow.
- The creator asked for the glow to be **more prominent**, so the freckles must read clearly even on the ivory background.
- Keep them points and clusters of light, not glowing skin or eyes.

**Range across the six:** vary these.
- **Freckle pattern:** scattered constellations; lines along the cheekbones and collarbones; dense on the hands and forearms; a mask-like band.
- **Glow colour:** cool green, pale blue, warm amber, white.
- **Eyes:** size and colour.
- **Adaptation to dark tunnels:** skin pallor, ear shape, long fingers, height, posture.

**Fresh departures (takes 5–6):** keep only "people of the deep tunnels whose skin carries points of light". Rethink the rest:
- body
- proportions
- how the light is arranged

One of them can push well away from human.

## PL3 · 03-made-people-lineup

**The people:**
- Bred or shaped by the Old Empire for war, through its forbidden research.
- Always Dominion. Whole and alive. Not undead: no rot, exposed bone, corpse pallor or glowing eyes.
- Joins healed long ago: no thread stitches, bolts or lab clichés.

**What failed before:**
- The refined take looked too human.
- The patchwork take, mismatched skin patches with seams, didn't land.
- The two closest attempts were a four-armed body and a body with grown horn plating and hoofed legs. Both were still not right.

**The silhouette must read as made at a glance**, as a people rather than monsters. Mostly men, since they were bred for war, with at least one woman.

**Six directions, one per cell:**
1. **Four-armed:** a broad, double-tiered chest, heavy upper arms and a smaller working pair below. Clean and deliberate, not grafted.
2. **Hoofed and plated:** fully committed, with digitigrade hoofed legs and grown horn plating along the shoulders and forearms. Integrated, not attached.
3. **The war breed:** every individual shares one bred look. A heavy bony brow, plated forearms, an oversized jaw and dense muscle. Uncanny because they're consistent, not because they're patched.
4. **Lengthened:** very tall and narrow, with an extra joint in the arms or legs and long reaching hands. Bred for reach.
5. **Grown armour:** a natural shell of bone or horn plates over the torso and limbs, like a beetle's or an armadillo's, with soft skin showing at the joints.
6. **Codex's own take:** a different idea from the five above. Note the reasoning in the manifest.

## PL4 · 04-new-peoples-lineup

**An exploration sheet:** six brand-new nonhuman peoples, one per cell, each a different idea.

**Ground each in a place or way of life**, so it feels like it belongs. One per cell:
1. high, cold mountains
2. dry scrub country
3. a cold, rocky coast
4. the poorest edges of a great city
5. deep forest canopy
6. open grassland

**Then design freely:**
- Each should differ from the others, and from humans, at a glance.
- They should still read as people: clothed, with tools or adornment and an intelligent face.

**Not:**
- elves or dwarves
- any rejected or retried people: Scions, Graftkin, Mourncaps, Lichenkin, Cairnfold, Glimmerfolk, ravenfolk, badgerfolk, mothfolk, rhinoceros folk, the mineral tripod
- the opaline people
- the existing peoples: lion folk, alderkin, riverfolk, goblins, orcs, giants

In the manifest, note each people's place and the core idea of their design.
