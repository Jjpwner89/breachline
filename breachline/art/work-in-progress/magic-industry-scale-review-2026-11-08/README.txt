CURRENT DIRECTION
People and creatures can cast directly. Enchanted items can hold power that users harness; fitted gauntlets are not the default.
Magic and industry can interact, but they are not inseparable. Avoid the repeated thread/ribbon treatment.
No mechanical/magitech hovering boats. Favor creatures or explicit flight magic for aerial travel.
The storm conduit's person-plus-amplifier concept remains interesting; its rejected appearance is not a reference.
Capacity, cost, prevalence and faction approaches remain open for review.

Exact prompts, revision prompts and textual decision history are in review-manifest.json. Built-in image generation was used.

LATEST CLARIFICATION
Magic was too item-dependent. Direct visible casting is not the required alternative. The firebox caster in the steam-crane revision is one possible interpretation, not a rule.
