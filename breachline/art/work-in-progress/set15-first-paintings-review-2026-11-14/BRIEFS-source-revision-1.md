# Breachline art briefs, set 15: revisiting the first paintings

Workflow: [briefs to paintings](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/BRIEF_WORKFLOW.md). Art direction: [art direction](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md).

- **Date and revision:** 2026-11-14, revision 1.
- **Source:** the creator reviewed the 61 card paintings unchanged since the first art batch (5 November) against the current art direction, in the [first paintings review](https://claude.ai/artifact/4CeQEHyMzrMBeT3vXMMyhc). They kept 46, marked 10 to revise and 5 to replace. The notes below are the creator's spoken notes from that review, 2026-11-14.
- **Scope:** 10 edits of approved paintings (R1–R10) and 5 new paintings to try against the current ones (N1–N5). The creator isn't sure every replacement will be used; the current painting stays in the game until one is picked.
- **Output stage:** finished card paintings at 1536 × 1024. Edits keep the original filename, with the previous version kept as history. Replacements are new files in the WIP folder until chosen.
- **WIP folder:** `art/work-in-progress/set15-first-paintings-review-2026-11-14/`.
- **Paths:** relative to `breachline/`. The paintings are `art/<file>.png`; card rules are in `engine.js`.
- **Status:** all items ready, none generated.

## Applies to the whole batch

- **Faction emblems where they mean something (creator, 2026-11-14).** The first paintings scatter a faction's emblem over flags, sashes, shoulders, medallions and weapons. The newer paintings use fewer, and the creator prefers that. Place an emblem where it reads clearly and makes sense, rather than on every surface. A unit whose allegiance matters, such as a Loyal Sunmane Oath soldier, should show it plainly.
- **Faces:** matte skin with simplified forms; no waxy shine. Don't fix a face with pores or surface noise.
- **Edits:** use the approved painting as the edit source and change only what the item names. Keep the composition, light, palette and brushwork unless the item says otherwise.

## Revisions of approved paintings

## R1 / spearman — Militia spearman
Type / alignment: unit / Vigil / Sunmane Oath. Source: `art/militia-spearman.png`.
- **Face:** less waxy. Keep the same young man.
- **Emblems:** he is Loyal to the Sunmane Oath, so the sun stays and should read plainly. At the moment small suns are scattered over his helmet, scarf, pennant and the flag behind. Consolidate them into one or two obvious ones, such as a bold sun on his scarf or tabard and the flag behind.
- **Tone:** slightly grittier, through earthier colour and a little more dust and wear. Not much darker.
Keep: the spear, the stance, the wheat field and the soldiers behind.
Design context — not for the image prompt: cheap Vigil soldier, stronger beside another Sunmane Oath unit.

## R2 / knight — Oath knight
Type / alignment: unit / Vigil / Sunmane Oath. Source: `art/oath-knight.png`. This is a new painting, not an edit.
The current painting has problems with the legs and the shield. Repaint it with a **male** knight, mostly facing the camera rather than turned away. He holds a narrow stone bridge or crossing: a man standing his ground, steady rather than mid-lunge.
Must keep: Sunmane Oath kit (plate cuirass, helmet, shield, sword), with the sun on the shield only. The shield-carrying hand sits behind the shield, out of sight. Trace hips through knees to feet: the legs must be coherent and grounded.
References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/09-vigil-equipment.png`, top row (required: Sunmane kit). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/people/01-humans.png` and `08-humans-additional-faces.png` (optional: faces).
Design context — not for the image prompt: Vigil unit with Holding ground; enemies that move next to it must stop. The Sallow Bridge link is a proposal in SETTING.md.

## R9 / saboteur — Thornwarden saboteur
Type / alignment: unit / Genesis / Thornwardens. Source: `art/thornwarden-saboteur.png`.
Her vibe works, but it isn't clear she is sabotaging anything: she seems to be handling a vine on a wooden wheel. Make the target and the harm readable. Suggested: the wheel is the water wheel of a loggers' sawmill, with the mill, cut timber or stumps in view, and the thorn growth she sets is visibly binding the spokes so the wheel is jammed. The Thornwardens have spent generations closing the old road and fighting logging, so a human work in the forest is the right kind of target. The staging can change if that reads better.
Keep: her identity, face, hair, clothes and gloves, and the river light.
Design context — not for the image prompt: Genesis unit with Ambush; it can enter beside or behind your units.

## R10 / sellsword — Sellsword
Type: unit / neutral. Source: `art/sellsword.png`.
He is a neutral card now, so he no longer belongs to the Warhorde.
- **Remove every three-spike emblem:** shoulder plate, cloak, and the banners and tent cloth behind.
- **Remove the red:** recolour the cloak and sash to undyed wool, brown or a faded neutral colour.
Keep: his face, the outfit and garments the creator likes (mail, the reused shoulder plate, the coin pouch), the sword, the rain and the brazier.

## Replacements to try

## N1 / chorister — Brass chorister
Type / alignment: unit / Gnosis / construct. Current: `art/brass-chorister.png`. Output: `brass-chorister-replacement-a.png` and `-b.png`.
The card stays a brass construct made by the Artificers, but the creator wants a new design for it. The current one is a thin, humanoid figure in teal enamel from the earliest art direction.
Paint two clearly different construct designs. Constructs follow narrow instructions and have personality without consciousness. Their anatomy can be abstract, asymmetric and shaped by the work they were built for, rather than an armoured person. Heads look like worked metal, never futuristic. Give it real weight and presence: something that could shove a person aside.
Must keep: brass and worked metal; a Gnosis setting such as an Artificer workshop or an Ambrel street. The name stays.
References: none required. The Brass siege-walker is a liked construct; carry forward its sense of believable, working machinery, not its design.
Design context — not for the image prompt: 4-cost Gnosis construct that pushes an adjacent enemy 1 tile. The lore codex's Artificers entry (L18) uses the current painting.

## N2 / clanraiders — Cinder Clan raiders
Type / alignment: unit / Fervor / Cinder Clans. Current: `art/cinder-clan-raiders.png`. Output: `cinder-clan-raiders-replacement.png`.
The current painting reads as people having fun, and the woman in front looks like a model. Paint a small band of raiders moving fast with purpose: lean, weathered, hard-used people with ordinary faces, mostly men. The Cinder Clans elect their leaders and can remove them; these are free raiders, not the Warhorde. A dusk or smoke-lit moment fits, such as crossing a stream or coming down a slope toward a raid, with the violence implied rather than shown.
Must keep: light gear suited to speed (short stitched jackets, hatchets, plain pouches). No glamour, no grins.
References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/14-raiders-and-berserker.png`, centre column (optional: raider kit). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/people/01-humans.png` and `08-humans-additional-faces.png` (optional: ordinary faces).
Design context — not for the image prompt: cheap, fast Fervor unit (Swift).

## N3 / goblin — Goblin cutter
Type / alignment: unit / Fervor / Warhorde. Current: `art/goblin-cutter.png`. Output: `goblin-cutter-replacement.png`.
The creator likes the current painting; this is a try at something different. Another fun goblin that is plainly aggressive: expressive, quick and pleased with itself, in the middle of a fight or a charge. For example, leaping from a cart onto the road with a cleaver in each hand, or rushing an enemy just out of frame. Humour is welcome if it doesn't strain. Attacks aren't aimed at the viewer.
Must keep: goblin anatomy from the reference sheet; scavenged, mismatched gear. It is Loyal to the Warhorde, so show the three spikes once where they read clearly.
References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/people/03-goblins.png` (required: anatomy). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/14-raiders-and-berserker.png`, left column (optional: kit).
Design context — not for the image prompt: cheap Fervor unit with Charge; stronger beside another Warhorde unit.

## N4 / raider — Orc raider
Type / alignment: unit / Fervor / Warhorde. Current: `art/orc-raider.png`. Output: `orc-raider-replacement.png`.
The current raider uses the older, more human, attractive orc design, and the creator doesn't want this card to be a woman. The burning background works: fire on the ground and fences behind give an implied raid. But the landscape's perspective is warped.
Paint a male orc raider charging through a burning farm edge. The background can stay abstracted and soft, out of focus, but its perspective must be consistent: one ground plane and one horizon. The violence stays implied.
Must keep: the current orc anatomy: coarse and visibly nonhuman, with a heavy brow, broad blunt nose, projecting jaw and irregular tusks. Not a green human. At most one small three-spike mark.
References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/people/04-orcs.png` (required: anatomy).
Design context — not for the image prompt: Fervor unit with Frenzy and Charge; it hits hardest on its first attack.

## N5 / warchief — Orc warchief
Type / alignment: unit / Fervor / Warhorde. Current: `art/orc-warchief.png`. Output: `orc-warchief-replacement.png`.
The current warchief looks like a character from World of Warcraft: the huge pauldrons, the oversized hammer and the emblem on every surface add up to that look. The three spikes can stay; they just shouldn't be everywhere. Paint a Warhorde warchief whose authority comes from presence and history: the Warhorde follows the strongest. He might be older and scarred, in salvaged, mismatched armour that fits the body, with a weapon of believable size, among followers at a camp. Find this world's own version of a warlord, not the familiar game one.
Must keep: the current orc anatomy (as N4). The three spikes, placed where they make sense.
References: `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/people/04-orcs.png` (required: anatomy). `art/reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/15-heavy-fighters-and-hired-blades.png`, left column (optional: salvaged cuirass).
Design context — not for the image prompt: Fervor leader with Warband; adjacent allies get +1 attack.
