Highneck · slight predator

The liked low body and raised neck remain, with a firmer jaw, more intent gaze and a small visible line of biting teeth. This suggests greater predatory capacity without changing the animal into an overtly threatening creature.

M21-23Preferred

Feathered heavyweight · revised face

The tall feathered build now has a compact solid muzzle and closed mouth, with the dangling mouthparts removed. Its feeding role remains open; this pass tests the revised face against the liked body and covering.

M21-24New exploration

Plateback

Overlapping dorsal plates shelter a low, deeply built animal with broad shoulders and large digging claws. A powerful burrower or root excavator is one possible role; its diet and the plates’ function are still proposals.

M21-25New exploration

Broadwhisker

A huge whiskered animal carries a deep chest on broad forefeet, with a rounded head and dense slate-and-cream covering. The wet footing suggests a possible waterside life, while its exact habitat and feeding behaviour remain open.

M21-26Preferred

Mantlewing

A long-bodied heavyweight combines strong walking limbs with broad folded membranes along its flanks and a thick balancing tail. The membranes are exploratory anatomy whose role remains unresolved; this picture does not establish that an animal this heavy can fly.

M21-27New exploration

Crownwool

A compact woolly heavyweight stands on broad split feet beneath a massive curled horn crown. This proposes a powerful highland animal, with its social behaviour, diet and exact horn arrangement still open.
