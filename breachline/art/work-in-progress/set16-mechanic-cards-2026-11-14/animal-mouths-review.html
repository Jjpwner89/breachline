M21-13 · v2

Latchmaw

Four muscular outer jaws end in hooked tips around a separate toothed central mouth. This revision proposes a predator that seizes and holds prey with its mandibles before biting, while retaining the russet animal’s body and mantle.

M21-14 · v2

Innerfang

The broad animal now has prominent outer canines and a smaller pair of toothed jaws extending from within its mouth. This proposes a second bite that grips prey and draws it inward; the exact reach and internal mechanics remain open.

M21-15 · v2

Splitbill

The divided beak now has a strong hook, hard cutting edges and an inner toothed grip. This version explores a more predatory feeding structure while preserving the animal’s long neck and copper-and-cream feathering.
