M20-1

Sodbrace

A broad grazer spreads its weight across six low legs beneath two layered back ridges. The proposal is that those ridges thicken with age, making established adults increasingly difficult to displace.

M20-2

Threestead

A deep, pear-shaped body rests on two forelegs and one central rear leg. Its lower body could become heavier as it matures in a rich meadow territory; the three-legged support is an anatomical proposal.

M20-3

Sillgrazer

Four short forelimbs brace a long body that rests on a broad creeping underside. This wet-ground grazer could become progressively wider and more sedentary as its feeding patch develops.

M20-4

Gravelcoil

A heavy, flexible browser settles into a loose coil, with small supporting legs along its flanks. The proposed mature animal occupies sheltered stony feeding sites, using its growing body mass to hold its ground.

M20-5Freeform

Hearthmantle · Selected

A low, broad animal rests beneath a deep russet-and-cream mantle, with debris and small growths caught in its covering. This open-ended proposal explores a creature whose long residence changes its coat; the nature of those growths remains undecided.

M20-6Freeform

Reedsettler

A feathered quadruped feeds in shallow water on four spreading webbed feet. The proposal is a wetland resident that grows denser and more settled with maturity, retaining its long flexible feeding neck.

M21-1

Bridgeback

Six stout legs support a long, deep-bodied browser with a low-reaching head. Its level trunk and distributed support propose a calm heavyweight built for moving substantial mass across open ground.

M21-2

Gatebreast

An upright browser holds a branch in short forelimbs above immense haunches and a heavy balancing tail. Its compact beak and dense coat frame a powerful but unhurried animal; habitat and exact size remain proposals.

M21-3

Mirestead

A very broad pale animal carries its weight on eight short legs. This proposes a heavyweight suited to soft ground, with a wide support base and an ordinary, calm grazing head.

M21-4

Redshoulder

Two enormous forelegs carry a high chest while four shorter rear legs support the lower hindquarters. The fur-to-hide transition and forward concentration of strength propose a formidable woodland-edge browser.

M21-5Freeform

Highneck

A massive animal rests with its long neck raised above gathered forelimbs and a broad low body. This independent proposal explores contained strength through a seated silhouette rather than a threat display.

M21-6Freeform

Bankward

A deep-bodied blue-and-cream grazer lowers its long neck toward wetland plants. Its broad feet and substantial torso propose a much heavier animal than the Salt-flat migrant; its ecology and precise scale remain open.
