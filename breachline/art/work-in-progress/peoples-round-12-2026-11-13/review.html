Saltreach · weathered coast

Accepted · Saltreach

Saltreach is a human coastal culture, distinguished by oiled wool, practical sea gear, and bone or shell ornaments. Their faces are now rougher and slightly sunburnt, with uneven pigmentation, chapped lips, and weathering expressed in broad painted forms rather than glossy or hyperdetailed skin.

W3

The Last Boat Up

Accepted · Saltreach

Saltreach fishers haul a small boat above the tide with a wooden roller and a taut rope. Their mildly sunburnt faces, rough skin, oiled wool, and modest shell ornaments carry the culture; the scene proposes a coastal livelihood rather than assigning every Saltreach person the same role or alignment.
