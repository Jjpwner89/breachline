## Formats

**Sheet format** follows accepted sheet C6:
- Reference: `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/active-scenes-and-people-2026-11-11/12-riverfolk-exploration.png`.
- Warm ivory empty background.
- Top two thirds: three full-body views (front, side, back) of one representative adult, head to feet visible.
- Lower third: four larger portraits of different adults of the same people, varied in age and face.
- Simple practical neutral cloth, unless the brief says otherwise. No labels, text or frames.
- Matte, diffuse painted treatment with coherent light and shadow shapes. Avoid wet shine, plastic gloss, hyperdetail and scratchy noise.

**Scene format:**
- Use the shared painting direction block in [ART_BRIEFS_SET11.md](../../ART_BRIEFS_SET11.md) ("Shared painting direction") unchanged, but swap its first lines for: concept painting, no card.
- Each brief begins with what should make the painting worth looking at; compose for that.

## The made people: Scions and Graftkin

**Setting background, for every S and G brief:**
- The Old Empire bred or shaped these people for war in its forbidden research, over 150 years ago.
- Today they are a people with families and generations. They are **always Dominion**, and Dominion is cruel and oppressive.
- They are whole living bodies and minds. **They are not undead:** no rot, no exposed bone, no corpse pallor, no glowing eyes.

The two takes differ on purpose:
- **Scions:** refined. The shaping barely shows and is unsettling for how controlled it is.
- **Graftkin:** rougher. Visibly built from joined parts, with long-healed seams. They are closer to Frankenstein's creature than Scions are, but use **no thread stitches, no bolts, no green skin and no lab clichés**. The grafts healed long ago, the way scar tissue heals.

Both should look like people the creator could imagine on cards, not monsters.

Dominion references, for mood only: `../../world-concepts-2026-11-08/01-the-price-of-morning.png`, `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/middle-setting-round-07-2026-11-09/10-the-levy-hall-v2.png`. Dominion palette options: ash grey, bone, tarnished silver, deep violet, cold overcast light.

### S1 · 01-scion-reference-sheet (sheet format)
**Appeal:** a people who are almost too right.
- Tall, long-limbed adults with slightly too-symmetrical faces and an even, controlled bearing.
- Faint old graft lines like grain in wood: across a cheekbone, down the throat, around a wrist.
- The four portraits run from almost human to clearly made, one step at a time. Experiment with what the made end looks like: eye colour, skin tone change across a seam, a small shaped ridge at the brow or jaw.
- Plain dark Dominion clothing instead of ivory cloth.

### S2 · 02-scion-at-court (scene)
**Appeal:** cold elegance and the cruelty underneath it.
- A Scion noble at ease in a cold, high Dominion hall, seated or leaning, attending to a letter or a cup.
- Human servants nearby keep their eyes down.
- The Scion is the clear focal subject. The cruelty is in the room's arrangement, not in violence.
- Reference for court mood: `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/world-edges-round-10-2026-11-10/07-the-velvet-stair-v2.png`.

### S3 · 03-scion-war-line (scene)
**Appeal:** uncanny sameness.
- A marching column of Scion soldiers (men) in cold rain, seen from the side or at an angle down the line.
- All the same height and build, with the same stride. The uniformity is the unsettling idea; one or two faces read clearly.
- Practical dark armour. The Black enamel kit is one option for one character, not a uniform for all: `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/middle-setting-round-07-2026-11-09/18-black-enamel-field-kit.png`.

### S4 · 04-taught-the-blade (scene)
**Appeal:** an inheritance passed on.
- A Scion parent correcting a Scion child's grip on a practice sword, in a bare yard or a stone room.
- Generations after the Empire, they still raise their children for war.
- Quiet, intimate and a little chilling. The child is clearly a child, with the faint marks of the people.

### S5 · 05-the-old-breeding-hall (scene, experiment)
**Appeal:** a holy place that should not be one.
- Scions visiting the ruin of an imperial hall where their ancestors were made. Old basins, channels or alcoves, half buried and overgrown.
- They treat it with reverence: lamps, offerings, heads bowed.
- The ruin is ordinary old-Empire stone, not glowing machinery.
- A larger setting with small figures is welcome.

### G1 · 06-graftkin-reference-sheet (sheet format)
**Appeal:** made from parts, yet whole and alive.
- Adults whose bodies show their making: one forearm heavier than the other, skin in patches of different tones meeting at thick healed seams, a bony growth at a shoulder or brow.
- Each of the four portraits is built differently, so the people don't share one template.
- Plain rough clothing.

### G2 · 07-graftkin-in-the-yard (scene)
**Appeal:** rough power under someone else's control.
- Graftkin war-hands (men) drilling with heavy weapons in a muddy Dominion yard.
- A human or Scion overseer watches from a raised walk.
- Energy, weight and mud. Overseer and setting are secondary.

### G3 · 08-the-hauling-gang (scene)
**Appeal:** bodies made to be used.
- A Graftkin gang hauling stone or a barrow cart up a grade in harsh light, ropes taut.
- Mixed men and women labourers.
- Their different builds show in how each one pulls.

### G4 · 09-graftkin-elder (scene or portrait, experiment)
**Appeal:** age shows differently on each part.
- An old Graftkin whose grafts have weathered at different rates: one hand spotted and thin, another still strong.
- A second, smaller pair of hands below the first.
- A portrait by a window or fire, absorbed in some small task suited to four hands.

### G5 · 10-beast-grafted (scene or portrait, experiment)
**Appeal:** how far the Empire went.
- A Graftkin with animal parts grafted on as well as human ones: horn plating across the shoulders, a hoofed leg, or another choice that reads clearly.
- Still a person with a face and intent, not a monster.
- Choose a setting and action that suit the figure.

## Riverfolk

**Setting background, for every R brief:**
- Riverfolk are human-proportioned, with fin-like ears, gill folds on the neck and patches of scale.
- Accepted anatomy: `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/active-scenes-and-people-2026-11-11/12-riverfolk-exploration.png`. Keep that anatomy and vary faces, ages, builds and clothes beyond it.
- They are split between Gnosis (Ambrel's waterworks, hydrology, charting) and Genesis (marsh and riverside villages).
- They are generally less privileged. They are fairly common, but live only in small villages or on the edges of cities, never a city or large town of their own.
- **They do not dive for mana-stones.** Their diving maintains canals, locks, sluices and channels.
- Skin is matte when dry. Wet skin can catch light, but no glittering fish scales.

Ambrel references: `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/middle-setting-round-07-2026-11-09/08-ambrel-stair-market.png`, `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/concept-round-08-2026-11-09/09-third-arch-of-ambrel.png`.

### R1 · 11-morning-shift (scene)
**Appeal:** two worlds in one frame.
- Riverfolk canal divers surfacing from an Ambrel lock at dawn, tools in hand, breath steaming.
- Above them, robed scholars cross a bridge without looking down.
- The divers are the focal subject. The bridge figures are smaller and quieter.

### R2 · 12-the-sounding (scene)
**Appeal:** absorbed, skilled work.
- A Riverfolk hydrologist on a flat boat on a wide grey river or estuary, dropping a weighted sounding line.
- A chart pinned under one knee, instruments simple and practical.
- Calm, specific concentration. Room for the water and sky.

### R3 · 13-stilt-village (scene)
**Appeal:** a small, lived-in home.
- A small Genesis Riverfolk marsh village on stilts at dusk: nets drying, low lamplight, kids jumping off a walkway into the water, an adult calling them in.
- Small by design: a handful of houses, not a town.
- Genesis village references, for society and craft only: `../../world-concepts-2026-11-08-set-02/02-voices-in-the-commons-brushwork-v2.png`, `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/world-edges-round-10-2026-11-10/03-the-rootbound-quarter.png`.

### R4 · 14-waiting-for-hire (scene)
**Appeal:** an ordinary, unfair morning.
- A row of Riverfolk shacks on the edge of a city by an outflow.
- A work crew waits on the bank for a foreman to call names, while the city rises beyond.
- Hardship without melodrama: people talking, someone eating, someone checking a diving hood.

### R5 · 15-mending-the-hood (portrait)
**Appeal:** a face that has worked.
- An older Riverfolk woman repairing a leather or waxed-cloth diving hood by window light.
- Gill folds and fin ears clearly visible.
- A specific, characterful face drawn from the face sheets, not a model face.

### R6 · 16-under-the-gate (scene, experiment)
**Appeal:** light through murky water.
- Seen from underwater: a Riverfolk diver clearing silt from a stone sluice gate.
- Daylight falls in shafts through green-brown water. Silt drifts, a rope runs up to the surface.
- Painted, not photographic. Keep the figure and gate clear at small size.

## Possible new peoples (P1–P6)

These test whether a people belongs in the setting at all. Use the sheet format unless the brief says otherwise. Invent freely beyond each description: anatomy, clothing and faces are open.

### P1 · 17-glimmerfolk (sheet format)
- Pale people with large eyes and freckles that faintly glow.
- They live in the old imperial tunnels and mines and know the underground better than anyone. Tentatively Gnosis-leaning.
- Adapted to dim light. Practical clothing suited to tunnels.
- Give the glow restraint: small, cool and natural, not neon.

### P2 · 18-mourncaps (sheet format)
- People with fungal skin, such as gill-like folds, cap-like growths or soft mycelial texture, who perform Genesis death rites and help the dead return to the soil.
- Gentle and solemn, not horror.
- Earthy colour with surprising accents.
- Simple ritual garments instead of ivory cloth.

### P4 · 20-lichenkin (sheet format, experiment)
- Small people with lichen growing on their skin, living alongside the alderkin.
- Moss-green and grey-gold.
- Include an alderkin silhouette in the background of the sheet for scale: `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/people/06-alderkin.png`.

### P5 · 21-saltreach (sheet format, human culture)
- A human culture, not a new species: harsh coastal people with weathered, salt-burned skin and bone and shell jewellery.
- This tests whether new human cultures can do some of the work of new races.
- Instead of ivory cloth, show their real clothing: oiled wool, layered wraps, practical sea gear.
- Faces from the human sheets, varied in age.

### P6 · 22-wildcard (sheet format)
- Invent one original intelligent people that fits this setting: a world 150 years after the Old Empire fell and the gods went silent, with five alignments (Vigil, Gnosis, Dominion, Fervor, Genesis).
- Not an elf, not a dwarf, and none of the cut species listed above.
- Surprise the creator. Note your reasoning in the manifest's `intent` field.
