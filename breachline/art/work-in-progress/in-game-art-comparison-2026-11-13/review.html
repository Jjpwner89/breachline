Does the approach carry over?

Eight paintings already in the game, revisited with coherent light, selective highlights and simpler painted forms.

IG1–2 have explicit written reservations. IG3–8 are the six lowest-ranked current pieces in your saved comparisons. Each of those six has only two comparisons and earlier praise; none is being treated as rejected.

Original compositions and identities are largely preserved so you can judge the rendering change. Every revision is unreviewed. Your game still uses the originals.
What this comparison can tell us

The test carries forward the recent approach: keep lively local colour, let light come from a credible source, simplify small surface detail, and preserve meaningful gestures and contact. It does not assume every scene should be darker or quieter.

These revisions change light and brush treatment together, so they cannot isolate a single cause. Harmonic Burst also reduces its visible pressure effect; Alderkin Elder has a small head-orientation change. Some revisions are darker: decide whether improved light also improves the whole painting.

Selection checked 226 live art mappings; 150 had saved ranking entries. Scores are relative to the pieces they faced, not a reliable absolute dislike scale.
