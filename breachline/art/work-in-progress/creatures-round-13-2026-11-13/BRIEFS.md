# Creatures round 13: briefs (2026-11-13)

20 creature concept paintings, approved by the creator on 2026-11-13 for generation.

**What the creator wants:**
- Dynamic, interesting creatures.
- Ribbonvane and the Slateprowler are the bar. Use them for quality and for the sense of an unfamiliar body caught mid-movement, not as templates:
  - `../../white-flier.png`
  - `../../concepts/slateprowler-on-the-descent.png`
- The earlier Genesis creatures (stag, herd bull, wolves, grazer) are too recognisable as real animals. In this round, invent bodies that don't read as a known animal with a twist.

**The ecological idea behind the round.** Life here is tied together:
- creatures depend on each other, prey on each other and live on each other
- life cycles change bodies
- people get by using living things as tools
- beauty and danger are often the same thing

Let each painting suggest a piece of a bigger living system, not a lone monster.

The creator refines ideas by seeing them, so experiment within each brief. Everything here is exploratory. A painting does not make a creature, behaviour or place canon until the creator accepts it.

## Shared notes for this round

**Movement and behaviour.** Show each creature doing something: hunting, feeding, launching, moulting, migrating, being used. Avoid:
- a posed portrait
- a centred animal staring at the camera
- a museum plate

**Invented but coherent anatomy:**
- Design each body from what it does: how it moves, eats, breathes, breeds and defends itself.
- Asymmetry, odd limb counts, plant- or fungus-like traits and colonial bodies are all welcome.
- The anatomy must still feel like it could work.
- Avoid the stock fantasy kit of dragon heads, insect mandibles on everything, or a mammal with extra eyes.

**Scale:**
- Most creatures are small to large.
- Only CR17 and CR18 are huge, and those are rare wonders people would stop to stare at.
- Don't inflate anything else.

**Colour and light:**
- Vary them across the batch: dawn, noon, storm, night, underwater, snow, haze.
- Not everything should be green, and not everything should be bioluminescent.
- Transmitted light through membranes is a strength of this world's creatures, as in the Dawnsail (`../../concepts/dawnsail-passage.png`) and Ribbonvane.

**Painting:**
- A traditional painted finish with varied detail: the creature's key feature is crisp and the surroundings are quieter.
- No photographic wildlife look and no 3D render.

**People:** where people appear, they're secondary, ordinary and drawn from the face sheets. Aggressive fighters, if any, are mostly men.

**Not in this round:**
- dragons or drakes
- animal-folk peoples
- the cut species (ravenfolk, badgerfolk, mothfolk, the mineral tripod, rhinoceros folk)
- recolours of Coalback, Reedcrown, Raincup or Tidevault

## Creatures that depend on each other

### CR1 · 01-hivebloom
**Appeal:** a predator made of many small lives.
- Hundreds of small fliers, each like a winged seed, flock into one large hunting shape.
- Show the moment it forms around prey, or bursts apart like blown seed heads when struck.
- The individual fliers are simple. The shape they make together is the creature.
- Open grassland or a forest edge, wind visible in the grass.

### CR2 · 02-seedhunters
**Appeal:** one life cycle in one image.
- A rooted plant-creature fires seed pods into the air.
- In the air, the pods unfold into small fliers that hunt insects.
- Nearby, a few have landed and are taking root.
- Read left to right or near to far so the cycle is clear without text.

### CR3 · 03-the-cleaning
**Appeal:** a quiet scene with a secret.
- A large plated grazer lies still in shallow water, eyes half closed.
- Long-fingered little creatures move over it, cleaning between its plates.
- Look closely and one of them is cutting a strip of flesh away while the grazer doesn't notice.
- The grazer's body is invented, not a hippo or rhino with plates.

### CR4 · 04-the-lure-field
**Appeal:** beauty that is a trap.
- A flowering meadow where some of the "flowers" are lures on long tongues from something buried beneath.
- An unfamiliar grazing animal steps in, just as the ground begins to lift and split around it.
- Catch the instant before the strike. The buried creature is only partly seen.

## Strange life cycles

### CR5 · 05-moltwalker
**Appeal:** a body leaving itself behind.
- A creature climbing out of its old body, soft and pale, with the new shell not yet hard.
- The empty husk still takes a few more steps on its own.
- Wet, raw and a little unsettling, but more wondrous than gross.
- A rocky place or a dim forest floor.

### CR6 · 06-wanderroots
**Appeal:** a slow migration under the moon.
- Tree-like organisms have pulled up their roots and are walking to new ground, a slow column crossing a valley at night.
- Roots used as legs, soil falling from them.
- One village light in the distance gives scale.
- Not alderkin: these aren't people, and they don't have faces.

### CR7 · 07-bloomfall
**Appeal:** death becoming life, fast.
- A large creature has died and is becoming a garden.
- Scavengers feed on it while sprouts, fungus and fruiting growths take it over.
- Show succession in one frame: fresher at one end, overgrown at the other.
- Beautiful rather than gory. This matches Genesis's belief that death should reclaim the body.

## Dynamic hunters and movers

### CR8 · 08-screeswimmer
**Appeal:** solid ground behaving like water.
- A predator moving through a loose rock slide as if swimming.
- Only its fins and the line of its back break the surface, with stones flowing around it.
- A mountain slope in hard light. A person or pack animal on the path above for scale and tension.

### CR9 · 09-tetherhunter
**Appeal:** a swing through open air.
- A cliff creature has shot a sticky line and is mid-swing out from the rock face to take prey off a ledge.
- A dynamic arc and a big drop below.
- The line is organic, like silk or a tongue, and not glowing.

### CR10 · 10-galeskater
**Appeal:** speed on ice.
- A flat, diamond-shaped creature skating across a frozen lake, a sail membrane raised to catch the wind.
- Low winter sun through the sail. Ice spray off its edges.
- Several in the distance, maybe racing.

### CR11 · 11-stormfeeder
**Appeal:** something living inside a storm.
- A filter-feeder that lives inside thunderheads, dropping through the cloud base as lightning cracks.
- Its body crackles faintly.
- Huge dark sky and rain curtains. A small hill or tower below for scale.
- The creature is large but not colossal.

### CR12 · 12-breakrider
**Appeal:** launching off a wave.
- A long, finned creature rides a breaking wave and launches into the air off the crest.
- Spray, green water lit from behind, a rocky coast.
- Its fins are spread wide in the air.

## Creatures people use

### CR13 · 13-lampkeepers
**Appeal:** living light in an ordinary city night.
- Small creatures glow when fed, kept in net cages hung along an Ambrel canal at night.
- A keeper feeds one, and it brightens.
- Warm, everyday and a little strange. Ambrel references:
  - `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/middle-setting-round-07-2026-11-09/08-ambrel-stair-market.png`
  - `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/concept-round-08-2026-11-09/09-third-arch-of-ambrel.png`

### CR14 · 14-mendergrubs
**Appeal:** healing that makes you flinch.
- A healer lays a pale, many-legged creature on a wound, and its legs draw the edges of the cut together.
- The patient looks away.
- Clean, careful and candlelit: a real treatment, not horror. Vigil-leaning.

### CR15 · 15-breath-sacs
**Appeal:** a tool that is alive.
- Underwater, a riverfolk diver breathes from a living, translucent bladder strapped to the chest, attached to a canal wall at work.
- Riverfolk anatomy: `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/active-scenes-and-people-2026-11-11/12-riverfolk-exploration.png`.
- Riverfolk dive to maintain canals, never for mana-stones.

### CR16 · 16-silkspinners
**Appeal:** a harvest from a burrow.
- At a Genesis village edge, people harvest sheets of cloth-like silk that a burrowing creature spins across its tunnel mouths.
- The creature peeks out, watchful.
- Morning light through the silk sheets. Village references, for society and craft only:
  - `../../world-concepts-2026-11-08-set-02/01-houses-above-the-ferns-brushwork-v2.png`
  - `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/world-edges-round-10-2026-11-10/03-the-rootbound-quarter.png`

## Rare wonders

### CR17 · 17-skyreef
**Appeal:** a reef in the sky.
- A coral-like colony drifting high in the air, with small fliers darting in and out of it.
- Seen from a mountain pass, where a traveller has stopped to stare.
- Light through the reef's thin branches. Rare and wondrous.

### CR18 · 18-the-breathing-hill
**Appeal:** the ordinary next to the impossible.
- A grassy hill beside a small village rises and falls slowly, once an hour.
- People go about their day: washing, herding, a child running up its slope. Everyone knows it's alive.
- Catch a tell: the grass shifting, a seam opening on its side.

## Setting-specific

### CR19 · 19-soulgleaners
**Appeal:** grief and hunger in the same place.
- Faint, translucent creatures gather around someone who has just died, feeding on the soul as it leaks away.
- In this setting a soul drains from the body after death. The setting does not say what that looks like, so keep it subtle and don't diagram it.
- Mourners may or may not see the creatures. Dominion-leaning.
- Cold light, quiet.

### CR20 · 20-ashlicks
**Appeal:** scavengers at the end of an industry.
- Scavengers crawl over a heap of burned-out mana-stones dumped near a great works, licking up the last traces.
- The Mountain's Lung burns stored mana from stones, and they're left permanently spent. These creatures live on whatever's left.
- Smoky, industrial light. A worker in the distance. Reference: `../../reference-library-2026-11-06/concept-round-08-2026-11-09/11-the-mountains-lung.png`.
