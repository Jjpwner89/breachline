A name for its presence

Veilwing remains the working name until you choose. Highsail is my leading suggestion.

  • Highsail — Graceful, memorable and tied to its silhouette and height.
  • Longwake — More stately; evokes the passage of an immense animal.
  • Sailback — An earthy common name that people in the world might use.
  • Cloudsail — Softer and more lyrical, with an immediate aerial association.
  • Orren — A short species name without describing a power or body part.
