# Breachline card art briefs: set 12, the new peoples

Follow the [brief workflow](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/BRIEF_WORKFLOW.md) and the [current art direction](../../.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md). Those two files govern the painting; these briefs only supply subject, lore and intent.

- **Source:** cards #192–215 in [CARD_PROPOSALS.md](../CARD_PROPOSALS.md), "Cards for the new peoples, 2026-11-13". The cards are proposals and not built yet. Lore comes from the new peoples sections of [SETTING.md](../SETTING.md).
- **Date and revision:** 2026-11-13, revision 2. Revision 2 applies the creator's notes of the same day: a new painting for #205 with the existing guard, a cave for #194, a trimmed #197, and new directions for #201, #202, #204 and #210.
- **Output stage:** card paintings. All six peoples here have final reference designs, so this batch goes straight to paintings rather than lineups.
- **Scope:** 20 new paintings, listed under "Briefs". Four more cards could use finished paintings that are already accepted; the creator decides on those first, and they get briefs only if declined.
- **Proposed WIP folder:** `work-in-progress/new-peoples-cards-2026-11-13/`
- **Paths:** relative to this file, `breachline/art/`.
- **Not included:** the Kitefolk, which the creator cut from the game on 2026-11-13. Don't use KR1 or the Kitefolk paintings.
- **Riverfolk references:** the files in `reference-library-2026-11-06/peoples-round-12-2026-11-13/` are accepted but, on 2026-11-13, still uncommitted in the main folder. Check that they're present before using them.

The batch covers six peoples. Within each people, vary place, weather, time of day, facing and camera distance, as the art direction asks. Several cards in one people shouldn't read as one scene repeated.

## Briefs

## 193 — Undercity edge-warden
Type / alignment / faction if established: Unit / Gnosis
Source and status: card #193; brief revision 1; ready
Output: painting; `work-in-progress/new-peoples-cards-2026-11-13/193-edgewarden.png`

Subject and setting:
A Glimmerfolk warden at the edge of the undercity. Here the developed districts give way to old sewers and stranger, unsafe places underground. Conflict at these edges is established lore. The warden's post and kit are proposals.

What makes it worth painting:
Someone who belongs to the dark, listening into it. A suggested scene: the last dressed stonework of a lived-in district stops at a rough opening. The warden waits there, ear-fans turned toward the black, glimmers low and calm.

Must keep:
GR1 anatomy: long neck, broad soft ear-fans, blue glimmers on matte dark skin, dotted arm lines spreading toward the hands. The glimmers must read at card size. The eyes don't glow. Their hearing is biology, so show no sound magic.

References:
`reference-library-2026-11-06/peoples-final-2026-11-13/02-glimmerfolk-final.png`, required for identity and anatomy.

Design context — not for the image prompt:
A blue unit with Overwatch: it strikes the first enemy to come into reach. See card #193.

## 194 — Undercity signaller
Type / alignment / faction if established: Unit / Gnosis
Source and status: card #194; brief revision 2; ready
Output: painting; `work-in-progress/new-peoples-cards-2026-11-13/194-signaller.png`

Subject and setting:
Glimmerfolk signal to each other by light in the dark. This is their biology, not a spell, and every pattern is unique. Their glow and sharp hearing make them excellent at finding their way underground. **The creator set this one in a cave, not the undercity (2026-11-13).** Some Glimmerfolk live in wild caves, which is established lore. This is one situation, not a change to their usual home.

What makes it worth painting:
A conversation made of light across a dark space. A suggested scene: two Glimmerfolk in a natural cave, some distance apart across a chamber or a drop. One brightens a pattern along their arm, and the other's glimmers answer.

Must keep:
**Two Glimmerfolk in a cave (creator, 2026-11-13).** GR1 anatomy, with clearly visible glimmers. Keep the signalling biological: no lanterns, beams or spell effects. The light comes from their skin.

References:
`reference-library-2026-11-06/peoples-final-2026-11-13/02-glimmerfolk-final.png`, required for identity and anatomy.

Design context — not for the image prompt:
A support unit that moves an ally from up to 3 tiles away. See card #194.

## 201 — Tithe-drinker
Type / alignment / faction if established: Unit / Dominion / Ashen Court
Source and status: card #201; brief revision 2; name proposed 2026-11-13; ready
Output: painting; `work-in-progress/new-peoples-cards-2026-11-13/201-tithedrinker.png`

Subject and setting:
Some Ashborn are made to draw life from others in a vampiric way. It's uncommon, and one of the ways Dominion spends the life it tithes, which is established lore. Very rarely an Ashborn rises to real power. This woman and her standing are proposals.

What makes it worth painting:
The one Ashborn with warmth in her face, attended by those who have none. A suggested scene: in a dim Court chamber, she sits while emptier Ashborn serve her, pouring, holding a basin or fastening a cuff. Their faces are blank and hers is almost alive.

Must keep:
**A woman, more attractive than the other Ashborn, and still clearly Ashborn, with some warmth in her face (creator, 2026-11-13).** Being served by emptier Ashborn is the creator's suggestion and can be re-staged. PL3D identity for everyone: cold pallor, hollow planes, restrained old scars and ordinary scale, with hers eased by that warmth. Not glowing veins or fangs; vampiric power stays rare and subtle. Dominion life stays harsh, so the attendants are spent, not pampered.

References:
`reference-library-2026-11-06/peoples-final-2026-11-13/04-made-people-final-lineup.png`, required for identity and anatomy.

Design context — not for the image prompt:
A stronger Bone harvester: it destroys an adjacent ally and gains +3 attack and +3 health. Don't show a victim being drained unless it's the best painting. See card #201.
