How different is the art direction for each alignment?

The oil/acrylic medium, painted detail, matte materials and coherent lighting rules apply to all five. The alignment section supplies subjects and palette/light suggestions. Those suggestions can steer the results when repeated too literally.

AlignmentSuggested colour and lightSubject emphasis
VigilGold, ivory, sky blue; warm lightProtection, duty, oaths and healing
FervorEmber, iron, soot, red; fire or storm lightIndependence, fighters and forceful magic
GnosisBlue, teal, brass, amber; lamplightLearned magic, scholarship and skilled craft
DominionAsh, bone, tarnished silver, deep violet; cold or overcast lightPower, oppressive service and the returned
GenesisMoss, bark, green, amber; dappled lightSocieties, households, nature and their defenders

The earlier prompts often amplified warm rims, glowing effects and dramatic staging outside Dominion. This round varies the situations and light direction while preserving colour and applying the same material treatment. The new scenes change several things at once, so your preferences will guide the next comparison rather than prove a single cause.
