## What these paintings are for

Each brief begins with what should make the painting worth looking at. Card rules are design records in CARD_PROPOSALS.md and stay out of image prompts: a Longshot unit needn't be shooting, and a spell needn't diagram its effect, unless that's the most beautiful choice. Spells can show a specific event with purposeful activity and restrained, scene-organizing magic, which the creator liked in Set 3. Large dramatic magic is welcome where the brief asks for it, as with Cinderfall.

## Shared painting direction

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Create one original Breachline fantasy PAINTING ONLY, landscape PNG 1536 x 1024, 3:2. No card frame, lettering, border, logo, watermark or signature.

PURPOSE: make a beautiful, distinctive image the creator will love and that belongs in this world. Choose the composition for its visual appeal: silhouette, light, material, colour relationships, atmosphere, character and imaginative detail. The image must work without a card title or rules text. A compelling presence, an enigmatic portrait, an animal in its habitat or a beautiful object can carry the whole painting. A small story or action is welcome when it improves the image; it is not compulsory.

MEDIUM: traditional oil and acrylic fantasy painting. Varied, visibly deliberate brushwork; coherent large light and shadow shapes; selective crisp accents and quieter lost edges. Believable weight and materials. Let paint describe forms instead of coating smooth 3D forms with a texture. Faces, hands, clothes and surroundings share a coherent painted language with varied local definition: focused features may be precise, while secondary forms use fewer quieter marks. Match brush scale to detail without camera blur. Give each material appropriate reflectance; avoid waxy skin and recurring rosy cheeks. Avoid glossy animation staging, rounded toy-like forms, exaggerated theatrical expressions, uncanny skin detail and uniform surface noise. Use ordinary reference-led proportions and varied faces, with occasional exceptional beauty rather than model casting throughout.

COMPOSITION: use the specific visual aim below. Allow quiet, eerie, austere, lyrical, humorous or forceful images as appropriate; appeal does not require spectacle or constant action. Use supporting elements only when they improve the composition. Imply most settings through a few well-chosen shapes; give a landscape breathing room when it is part of the appeal. Natural connected anatomy, simple coherent hands, readable object contacts and consistent scale. Leave space around important silhouettes.

MAGIC: subject-specific and visually interesting, often understated and assumed in the world. It can reside in a person, creature, material or object. Do not inflate a small, intriguing magical idea into an explosion or a diagram of its consequences. Do not default to luminous threads or ribbons; specific accepted uses remain valid.

CONTINUITY: where accepted references are attached, they establish identity, anatomy and signature equipment. Preserve their appealing design, proportions and material character while composing a finished painting. For new characters, consult ordinary face and species sheets for varied proportions, then invent beyond them; exact reuse is welcome when requested, not a closed cast.

SETTING: high fantasy about 150 years after the Old Empire collapsed and the gods fell silent. New kingdoms and ordinary lives persist through hard times, with real but hard-won hope. Communities have their own local populations. Orcs are never Vigil, and lion folk keep to themselves. Gnosis explores scholarly magic and ingenious craft. Dominion life is harsh and oppressive. Fervor values passion and freedom, with fire and storm magic. Genesis includes societies as well as wilderness. Proposed scenes do not establish new lore.
```

## Variety across the batch

### 142. Focus-rifle marksman: focus-rifle-marksman.png

**Visual aim:** A marksman lying still on a slate roof with a long brass-and-glass focus rifle, a point of lantern light in its crystal.

**References:** `reference-library-2026-11-06/character-kits-2026-11-08/05-gnosis-foot-soldier.png`; `reference-library-2026-11-06/equipment/10-gnosis-equipment.png`; `reference-library-2026-11-06/design-directions-round-06-2026-11-08/11-underbridge-vaporwright.png`

A male Artificer marksman lies prone along a slate roof ridge above Ambrel's misty streets, cheek to the stock of a long rifle of brass, dark wood and glass. It is a focus for magical energy, not a gunpowder weapon: no smoke, no cartridges, a small steady point of lantern light gathered in a crystal near the muzzle. Morning haze, chimneys, his patient stillness. Guns stay rare in this world, so the rifle should look like a crafted instrument.

### 145. White morning: white-morning.png

**Visual aim:** A broad winter field at pink-gold dawn, an irregular advance caught as branching rime spreads through the soldiers' path.

**References:** `reference-library-2026-11-06/magic-studies-round-04-2026-11-08/02-rime-drake.png`; `frost.png`

A wide winter field under a pink-gold dawn sky. Six soldiers advance in a loose irregular group at different depths, with uneven spacing and varied orientations. A nearer soldier twists down toward a boot caught by frost; farther men brace, turn to warn each other or halt mid-step. A separate small Conclave caster directs a low fan of feathered rime through furrows and stubble, catching their legs where they were walking. Large blue-white landscape shapes, natural perspective and clear ground contact. No posed row, repeated stance or ruler-straight frost strip.

**Review:** The original evenly spaced row was rejected as implausible. This direction supersedes the straight-line staging. The creator approved the revised painting: “that's better on the frost one”.

## Fervor

### 104. Kindle the line: kindle-the-line.png

**Visual aim:** Firelight climbing a line of waiting fighters at dusk, their faces lit from below, calm inside the flame.

**References:** `cinderfall.png`; `reference-library-2026-11-06/magic-and-craft-2026-11-08/07-cinder-cant-drummer.png`; `reference-library-2026-11-06/concept-round-08-2026-11-09/13-cinderwake.png`

A Cinder Clans fire-singer, an older man with a soot-dark beard, drags the burning head of a staff through dry grass in front of a line of mostly male clan fighters on a ridge at dusk. Fire runs along the line and climbs their legs and shoulders without catching their clothes; ember-glow gathers in the seams of their armour and in their eyes. The flames and the dark violet sky carry the painting. Keep the fighters' faces individual and the fire's shapes large and readable, not a wall of noise.

### 105. Slagheart hulk: slagheart-hulk.png

**Visual aim:** A monumental elemental of cooling rock and magma, slow and heavy, in a volcanic pass with a small human scale cue.

**References:** `reference-library-2026-11-06/magic-studies-round-05-2026-11-08/06-brazen-gatebeast.png`; `reference-library-2026-11-06/concept-round-08-2026-11-09/04-coalbacks-first-frost.png`; `cinderfall.png`

A huge creature of black cooling crust and orange molten seams walks a cinder pass, its body broad and unhuman: no knightly armour, no human face, more boulder and lava flow than person. Where the crust cracks, molten light shows; steam rises where drizzle meets it. A goatherd and two goats on the far slope give its scale. Hot interior light against cool grey rain and stone. The creator approved this creature painting as final card art; further lore and mechanics are not inferred.
