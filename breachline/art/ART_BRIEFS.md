**For new images, don't use the style block below.** It asks for more drama than the creator wants. Follow `.claude/skills/art-direction/SKILL.md` and the style block in `ART_BRIEFS_SET2.md`.

This file holds art briefs for all 19 cards in the current set of Breachline, an original fantasy card game.

## Instructions for the image generator

1. Generate one image per card, using the style block below plus that card's prompt.
2. Make each image a landscape PNG at about 3:2, for example 1536 x 1024.
3. Save each image in this `art/` folder under the file name given in its heading.
4. Skip any card whose image file already exists, so a run can be resumed or a single card regenerated by deleting its file.
5. Use relevant approved references for specific qualities while varying composition, palette, light and casting; the first painting is not a template for the batch.
6. Change only the files in this `art/` folder. Don't edit the game code or these briefs.
7. If you can't generate images in this environment, stop and say so. Don't substitute placeholders, drawings made in code, or images from the web.

When finished, list the files you created and any cards you couldn't complete.

## Style block

Apply this to every image:

```text
You are painting card art for an original fantasy card game called Breachline. Apply this style to every image.

Medium: traditional oil and acrylic fantasy illustration on canvas, with visible, confident brushwork and rich painted texture. It must never look like a photograph, a 3D render, anime, a comic or flat vector art.

Detail: highly detailed armour, cloth, fur, scales, stone and weather. Realistic anatomy and weight, with faces full of character.

Composition: landscape format, about 3:2. One clear focal subject that reads instantly at small size, placed with a strong silhouette against a simpler background. Keep important details away from the outer edges, because the image will be cropped into a card frame.

Motion: favour dynamic poses and diagonal lines, such as lunging bodies, low heroic camera angles, flying cloth, sparks, dust and debris caught mid-air. Show the split second of greatest action.

Light and scale: dramatic directional light with strong contrast, atmospheric depth and a sense of epic scale.

Do not include: text, letters, card frames, borders, logos, watermarks, signatures, user interface elements, or characters and symbols from existing games, films or books.
```

Most units and spells should be in motion. Lands and a few calm characters can be still, as each prompt specifies.

## Lands

Land art has no characters, so its drama comes from scale, weather and light.

### Sunlit plains: sunlit-plains.png

*White land. Adds 1 mana each turn.*

Paint a vast golden grassland at sunrise, the grass rippling in waves under a strong wind. Shafts of warm light break through towering clouds and sweep across the plain. In the far distance, a white stone keep stands on a low rise, with long ivory and gold banners streaming from its towers. An ancient paved road cuts diagonally through the grass toward it. The mood is hopeful and immense. No people in view.

### Cinder peaks: cinder-peaks.png

*Red land. Adds 1 mana each turn.*

Paint a jagged range of black volcanic mountains at dusk, seen from a low angle so the peaks tower overhead. Rivers of molten lava glow orange down the slopes, and ash clouds lit from below roll across the sky. Sparks and embers blow across the foreground on a hot wind, past broken rock and a rusted war banner driven into the ground. The mood is violent and awe-inspiring. No people in view.

### Lantern quarter: lantern-quarter.png

*Blue land. Adds 1 mana each turn.*

Paint a dense fantasy city at night in heavy rain, seen from high on a rooftop looking down a canyon of tall stone towers, domes and brass spires. Hundreds of amber lanterns glow in the windows and hang on chains across the narrow streets, reflected on wet cobblestones and canals. Faint blue arcane light rises from an observatory in the distance. The mood is mysterious and scholarly. No people close enough to make out.

## The Sunmane Oath (white)

White art should feel heroic and hopeful: gold light, clean armour, and people protecting one another.

### Militia spearman: militia-spearman.png

*Unit. Cost 1. Attack 20, health 25.*

Paint a young human farmer turned soldier in a patched gambeson and a dented iron helmet, charging forward through trampled wheat with a long wooden spear thrust ahead of him. His mouth is open in a frightened, determined shout, his body leaning hard into the run. Other militia are a blur behind him under a simple white pennant. Morning light from the side, with dust and chaff kicked into the air. He should look brave but clearly not a veteran.

### Temple cleric: temple-cleric.png

*Unit. Cost 2. Attack 15, health 40. Ability: heal an adjacent ally 25.*

Paint a middle-aged human woman cleric in layered ivory robes and a gold-trimmed mantle, kneeling on a muddy battlefield with one hand pressed to the chest of a wounded soldier. Warm golden light pours from her palm and lights both faces from below, while her other hand grips a tall bronze staff planted in the ground. Her robes whip in the wind, and arrows and smoke fill the dim background. Her expression is calm and fierce. This is one of the stiller images, with all its energy in the light.

### Oath knight: oath-knight.png

*Unit. Cost 3. Attack 30, health 50.*

Paint a human knight in polished plate armour with a gold sunburst on the breastplate, seen from a low angle as she braces behind a tall kite shield. An unseen enemy's blow has just struck the shield, throwing out a burst of sparks and splinters, and her longsword is drawn back ready to answer. Her white cloak flies out behind her and her helmet visor is raised to show a scarred, resolute face. A stone bridge and storm clouds pierced by sunlight fill the background.

### Sunmane lancer: sunmane-lancer.png

*Unit. Cost 4. Attack 45, health 65. Swift. Ability: Lance through, which hits an enemy and the enemy behind it.*

Paint a powerful lion-headed warrior in gold and ivory plate armour, riding an armoured white warhorse at full gallop straight toward the viewer at a slight diagonal. His long lance has just punched through an enemy's raised shield and is driving on into the orc behind it, with shards of wood and metal exploding outward. His golden mane and crimson cape stream back, and his jaws are open in a roar. The horse's hooves are off the ground, throwing up clods of earth. Low camera angle, blazing sunrise behind him. This is the set's hero image, so make it the most thrilling.

### Pride warden: pride-warden.png

*Unit. Cost 6. Attack 60, health 95. Ability: Rally, giving adjacent allies +10 attack this turn.*

Paint a huge, elderly lion-headed champion in heavy ornate gold armour, with a greying mane and battle scars, standing on the crest of a shattered stone wall. He roars skyward with one arm flung high, holding up a great war standard whose ivory cloth snaps in the wind. Below him, smaller lion folk and human soldiers surge forward, weapons raised and inspired by him. Sunlight breaks through the smoke directly behind his head like a halo. Very low camera angle, so he looks monumental.

### Shield of dawn: shield-of-dawn.png

*Instant. Cost 1. When your unit is attacked, prevent 25 of that damage.*

Paint the instant a huge orc axe smashes down on a young human soldier who has fallen to one knee and thrown up an arm. The blade stops dead against a curved shield of pure golden light that has flared into being just above the soldier's arm, sending out a ring of radiant shards and blowing dust outward. The orc's arms shudder from the impact. Focus on the point of contact, with the shield as the brightest thing in the image, in a dim pre-dawn battlefield.

### Blessed banner: blessed-banner.png

*Enchantment. Cost 2. A unit gets +10 attack and +15 health.*

Paint a human soldier in worn armour lifting a long ivory and gold battle banner with both hands as it is touched by a shaft of sunlight from a parting sky. Gold motes rise from the cloth and swirl around the soldier, whose wounds are closing and whose eyes have lit with new strength. The banner billows huge across the upper part of the image, its surface blank apart from an abstract sunburst. Other soldiers turn to look in wonder. Do not paint any writing on the banner.

## The Warhorde (red)

Red art should feel hot, loud and reckless: firelight, smoke, and bodies thrown fully into the attack.

### Goblin cutter: goblin-cutter.png

*Unit. Cost 1. Attack 25, health 15.*

Paint a wiry green-grey goblin leaping through the air straight at the viewer with a jagged rusty cleaver in each hand, both raised for a wild downward chop. His huge ears are flattened back, his eyes are wide and his needle teeth are bared in a delighted grin. He wears scraps of stolen armour, tied on with rope. Behind him, sparks and smoke rise from a burning supply wagon. Extreme foreshortening, as though he will crash through the picture. He looks fast, vicious and very easy to kill.

### Orc raider: orc-raider.png

*Unit. Cost 2. Attack 35, health 30.*

Paint a lean, tusked orc in light leather and bone armour, sprinting downhill through the smoke of a burning village with a heavy notched axe swung back over one shoulder. Her body leans into the run, her braided black hair and a torn red war-sash fly behind her, and she bellows mid-stride. Burning thatch and sparks fill the air behind her, with a low orange light from the fires. She is all speed and aggression, with almost no armour.

### Sellsword: sellsword.png

*Unit. Cost 3. Attack 35, health 45.*

Paint a weathered human mercenary with a grey-streaked beard and mismatched armour taken from many armies, turning sharply in the middle of a brutal melee in a muddy war camp. He has just parried with a battered greatsword and is twisting to swing again, with rain and blood-red firelight glinting off the blade. A heavy coin purse swings at his belt and a defeated rival lies at his feet. His expression is bored and professional. The Warhorde's orcs fight around him, and he looks out of place among them.

### Orc warchief: orc-warchief.png

*Unit. Cost 4. Attack 55, health 50.*

Paint a massive orc warchief with great curved tusks, wearing a mantle of trophy skulls and heavy blackened iron plate, bringing a gigantic two-handed war hammer down onto the ground. The impact splits the earth, throwing rock and fire upward around him. Veins stand out on his arms, and he bellows with his head thrown forward. A horde of orcs with spiked banners charges past on both sides, with volcanic peaks glowing on the horizon. Low camera angle, showing raw power with little defence.

### Hill giant: hill-giant.png

*Unit. Cost 5. Attack 50, health 85.*

Paint an enormous shaggy giant, as tall as a castle tower, wading through a narrow rocky valley and hurling a boulder the size of a house with his whole body twisted into the throw. He wears crude hides and a belt of bones, with moss and small trees clinging to his shoulders. Tiny soldiers scatter in panic around his shins, giving a clear sense of scale. Heavy grey clouds and a ray of dusty sunlight. Very low camera angle, with the boulder sailing directly overhead.

### Elder dragon: elder-dragon.png

*Unit. Cost 7. Attack 85, health 100. Flying, Swift.*

Paint an ancient, colossal red-black dragon diving out of an ash-filled sky above a burning fortress, its huge wings folded half back and its neck stretched forward. A torrent of white-hot fire pours from its jaws and sweeps along the walls, while soldiers on the battlements look tiny against it. Every scale is scarred, and its horns are cracked with age. Strong diagonal composition from upper left to lower right, lit from below by its own flames. This is the set's largest creature and should feel utterly unstoppable.

### Firebolt: firebolt.png

*Instant. Cost 2. On the enemy turn, 25 damage to an enemy unit.*

Paint a spear of roaring fire streaking diagonally across a dark battlefield and striking a charging knight in the chest, bursting against his shield arm in a blast of embers. At the far end of the streak, a goblin shaman crouches on a rock with one arm flung out, his robes smoking and his grin lit orange. The knight is thrown backward off his feet. The bolt is the brightest element, with motion lines of flame and sparks trailing behind it.

### Withering hex: withering-hex.png

*Enchantment. Cost 2. An enemy unit gets -15 attack.*

Paint a proud human knight frozen mid-swing as his sword arm fails him. Sickly orange-green smoke winds around his arm in the shape of grasping fingers, and his steel blade is rusting and flaking away as we watch. His face shows sudden shock. In the shadows behind him, a hunched old orc witch with a necklace of bones twists a small knotted charm in her hands, her eyes glowing. Dim torchlight, with flakes of rust drifting through the air.

## The Lantern Conclave (blue)

Blue art should feel clever and uncanny: lamplight, rain, and magic that looks like precise knowledge. The Warhorde deck borrows this one card for now.

### Street conjurer: street-conjurer.png

*Unit. Cost 2. Attack 25, health 30. Ranged, attacking up to 2 tiles away.*

Paint a young human mage in a soaked dark blue student's coat with brass buttons, sliding to a stop on a wet cobbled alley at night with one foot skidding through a puddle. One hand flings a spinning lance of blue-white arcane light down the alley, and the other holds open a small book whose pages flutter wildly. Glowing geometric diagrams hang in the air around the raised hand. Amber lanterns overhead and streaks of rain catch the light. The expression is focused and a little reckless, as though this spell has only just been learned.
