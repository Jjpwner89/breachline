Final · creator approved

Art brief and inspection notes

A narrow mountain pass at sunrise: one straight band of gold light falls down the length of the pass from a notch in the peaks. Harmonic burst

A ring of struck air bursts outward from a properly suspended bronze bell in an Ambrel plaza.

Final · creator approved
Full painting · PNGGame JPEG
Art brief and inspection notes

In a sunlit Ambrel plaza, a bronze bell hangs mouth-down by wrought-iron crown straps beneath a braced oak headstock between two posts fixed in low stone sockets. Its lip is clear of the cobbles and free to vibrate. A Conclave wardwright has just struck its side; a ring of visible air pressure throws dust, leaves and paper outward, with two attackers secondary at its far edge. Preserve the noon light and clear bell-led composition.

Inspected corrected installation: two grounded timber posts support a braced crossbeam; wrought-iron straps connect the headstock to the crown loops. The bell hangs freely with clear space below its lip and an internal clapper. Previous lip-support stones are removed. Caster's arms, legs and grip remain connected; frame does not intersect the figure. Plaza, scale, pressure wave and secondary figures preserved.

Creator correction: the bell now hangs from its crown beneath a braced timber frame.

Creator final review: approved, but not a favorite. Prefer other mechanisms for Gnosis. No new sound-focused Gnosis cards before 250 total cards; after that, sound remains at most one in eight Gnosis cards, preferably fewer.
