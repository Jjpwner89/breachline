# Breachline Allure

A selective character treatment for visible adult attractiveness through figure, clothing, expression and presence. The creator requested this on 2026-11-14, naming Street Conjurer in the blue dress (the exact supplied image), Gilt-hall Temptress, A Door into Snow and Hostess of the Late Hour.

The default is **a slightly fuller, naturally supported bust and a lower, well-shaped neckline**, with the whole character remaining compelling. Make the change readable; do not dilute the request into facial beauty alone. The more pronounced courtly examples establish available range, not a minimum for every character.

## Shape the character

- **Proportions:** add modest fullness relative to the intended character's frame. Preserve believable weight, support, ribcage and shoulder relationships. For edits, keep the existing build, waist, limbs and age except where the requested change requires local adjustment. The slender frames in several references are examples, not an instruction to slim every body.
- **Neckline:** choose a lower scoop, broad square, sweetheart, soft V or softly draped opening that belongs to the garment. Visible cleavage, collarbones and an open neck/shoulder line can carry the cue. Use enough openness to read at card size while keeping opaque coverage and credible construction.
- **Clothing:** give the bodice plausible seams, tension, support, folds and attachment to the rest of the garment. Fine straps with small rings or clasps, soft gathers, lace, embroidery and off-shoulder layers are useful options. Vary fabric, cut, colour and decoration; a wine-coloured court gown is not the uniform. Keep the character's setting, climate, station and required equipment legible.
- **Face and presence:** retain a distinctive adult face and recognisable character. Attentiveness, composure, warmth, confidence or a knowing glance can support attraction. A competent mage absorbed in a task can be alluring without looking at the viewer or performing seduction.
- **Composition and paint:** let face, gesture, clothing and situation work together. Keep connected anatomy and a coherent pose. Describe shape through cloth and painted light, with the shared matte-skin treatment. Avoid a chest-only crop, exaggerated arching or changing the whole scene merely to display the adjustment.

This is clothed, non-explicit character art for adults. Ordinary glamour and visible cleavage are within the intended treatment; do not escalate it into nudity or an unrelated erotic scene.

## Calibrate the strength

Default to **understated allure**: a modest but visible increase in fullness, an open neckline and flattering tailoring integrated with the character's existing life.

For a deliberately glamorous courtier, hostess or similarly specified role, draw more strongly from the Temptress and Hostess: a more pronounced silhouette, lower gathered neckline, fine trim and assured presence. Preserve the requested degree rather than escalating each revision.

Use the selected Door into Snow for allure coexisting with a mage's identity, purposeful hands and a substantial setting. The creator-supplied blue-dress Street Conjurer offers relaxed daylight glamour: softly draped fabric, fine ring-fastened straps, a fitted embroidered waist and casual spellcasting. Neither requires a courtly invitation or a dark interior.

## Put it into a brief or edit

Add a compact presentation instruction to the subject or “Must keep” field. Keep mechanics separate and let the art agent choose a good painting. For example:

> Apply understated Breachline Allure to this adult mage: a slightly fuller natural bust, a lower softly squared neckline with visible cleavage, and fitted opaque clothing with believable support. Keep her distinctive face, existing build and attentive character; let her gesture and surroundings carry the scene.

For a local revision:

Adjust only the bust and the necessary bodice tailoring: modestly fuller natural proportions and a lower, well-constructed neckline. Keep the existing waist and limbs, garment palette and all unrelated details.
